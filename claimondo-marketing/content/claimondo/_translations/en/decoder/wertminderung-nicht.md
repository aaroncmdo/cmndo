# "No Diminished Value Has Occurred" – Decoder

> **In brief:** Insurers deny Wertminderung (diminished value) using three standard arguments — vehicle too old, minor damage, repair carried out professionally. The frequently cited rule of thumb "no diminished value after 5 years and 100,000 km" is **not a binding threshold** but an orientation value. Under BGH case law, Wertminderung is an independent damage claim that must be assessed on a case-by-case basis — high-value vehicles or well-maintained models can remain eligible for diminished-value claims well beyond these limits.

## Also Known As

Insurer strikes diminished value · Wertminderung denied · Diminished value reduced

---

## What You Have Just Received — Verbatim

Typical variations:

> *"No Wertminderung (diminished value) applies to your vehicle given its age of [X] years and mileage of [Y] km."*

> *"The repair was carried out professionally — no diminished value is apparent."*

> *"This is a minor (Bagatell) damage — Wertminderung does not apply."*

> *"Our appraiser sees no diminished value."*

Three recurring lines of argument:

- **Rule-of-thumb argument:** "No diminished value after 5 years / 100,000 km"
- **Minor-damage argument:** "Damage too small"
- **Professional-repair argument:** "The repair fully compensates for the loss in value"

All three are **not sustainable** as blanket grounds for refusal under BGH case law.

---

## What Is Really Behind This

### 1. Eliminating a damage position as a mass operation
Wertminderung typically amounts to **€500–€3,000** per case for mid-range vehicles. Blanket refusals are highly profitable when accepted without objection — insurers deliberately calculate on a high acceptance rate.

### 2. Misuse of the rule of thumb
The frequently cited thresholds of "5 years / 100,000 km" originate from older appraiser conventions. BGH case law rejects a rigid age limit — what matters is the **individual assessment** of the specific vehicle on the specific market.

### 3. Artificially raising the minor-damage threshold
Insurer letters routinely place the minor-damage (Bagatell) threshold at €3,000 or higher. Under BGH case law it typically sits at **€500–€1,500** — below that, Wertminderung is generally minimal but not necessarily zero.

### 4. Insurer appraiser sets a low figure
Where Wertminderung is not flatly refused, the insurer's appraiser sets it **significantly lower** than an independent expert would — differences of 50% to 200% are observed in practice.

---

## What Case Law Says

### Wertminderung as an independent damage position
The BGH recognises Wertminderung in consistent case law as an independent damage position under § 249 BGB (German Civil Code on damage compensation). It arises from the fact that **a vehicle declared as "accident-damaged" achieves lower prices on the used-car market** — even after a fully professional repair.

### Requirements
Three conditions must be met:

1. **Professional repair** of the accident damage
2. **Significant damage** — above the minor-damage threshold
3. **Loss of market acceptance** — a measurable loss in value

### No rigid age limit
The practical rule of thumb "up to 5 years and 100,000 km" is **not a BGH standard** but an appraiser orientation rooted in tradition. High-value premium vehicles or well-maintained models can give rise to a Wertminderung claim beyond these thresholds — individual assessment decides.

### Calculation methods in the expert report (Gutachten)
Recognised methods for calculating Wertminderung are:
- **Ruhkopf-Sahm method** (classic, widely used)
- **Halbgewachs method**
- **Modified Frankfurt Method (MFM)**
- **Berens-Hettberg-Strunk method**

Each method follows a different calculation logic. The chosen method must be substantiated in the Gutachten.

### Typical ranges (orientation)

| Damage amount | Wertminderung range |
|---|---|
| Under €1,500 | usually €0 (minor-damage range) |
| €1,500 – €5,000 | €200 – €700 |
| €5,000 – €15,000 | €500 – €2,000 |
| Over €15,000 | €1,500 – €5,000 |

→ These figures are **orientation values**, not rules. Structural damage, premium vehicles, or special circumstances can justify significantly higher Wertminderung claims.

## What You Do Now — Concretely

### 💡 The most important rule
Your own independent appraiser (Sachverständiger) is non-negotiable. Their Gutachten contains the Wertminderung calculation — without it you have no defensible position against the insurer's refusal.

### 🛠 Four steps

**1. Gutachten with Wertminderung calculation**
Instruct a BVSK-certified Sachverständiger — the Wertminderung must be explicitly quantified, with the method named.

**2. Counter the rule-of-thumb argument**
For a young or well-maintained vehicle: cite service history, brand-authorised servicing, and premium-model arguments in writing.

**3. Concrete minor-damage assessment**
State the exact damage amount. With damage above €1,500 the minor-damage argument typically does not hold.

**4. Litigation where the difference exceeds €500**
Where the insurer refuses or significantly undervalues the claim, legal escalation by our partner law firm is worthwhile. Legal costs are borne by the insurer.

## What the Insurer Will Likely Do Next

### Response A — "Our appraiser sees less"
> *"Our appraiser sees a maximum of €200 in Wertminderung."*

**Your response:** Demand a methodology comparison. Your own Gutachten carries equal weight. In the event of a dispute, a court-appointed appraiser decides.

### Response B — Sharpening the age argument
> *"At 7 years of age, Wertminderung is excluded."*

**Your response:** BGH case law on individual assessment. Premium brand / good condition / full service history are valid arguments against a rigid age threshold.

### Response C — Shifting the minor-damage threshold
> *"No Wertminderung applies to damages under €3,000."*

**Your response:** BGH case law on the minor-damage threshold. It sits typically at €500–€1,500, not €3,000. Cite the specific extent of damage (structural damage vs. paint damage is a significant distinction).

### Response D — Prior damage argument
> *"The vehicle already had prior damage — no additional Wertminderung."*

**Your response:** Eggshell-skull doctrine (Eierschalen-Schädel-Doktrin). Prior damage does not reduce the claim — the **causal contribution** of the current accident is to be assessed independently.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Wertminderung?**
The loss in value of a repaired vehicle on the used-car market because it is declared as "accident-damaged" — even after a fully professional repair. An independent damage position under § 249 BGB.

**Can I claim Wertminderung for my 7-year-old car?**
Possibly — the rule of thumb "no Wertminderung after 5 years" is not a BGH standard. For premium brands, good condition, and brand-authorised servicing, Wertminderung can be established beyond 5 years. Individual assessment by your own Sachverständiger required.

**From what damage amount does Wertminderung apply?**
Typically from €1,500 in repair costs — the minor-damage threshold is not €3,000, as insurers frequently claim. For structural damage, it may apply from lower amounts.

**Who calculates the Wertminderung?**
Your own Sachverständiger in the Gutachten, using recognised methods (Ruhkopf-Sahm, Halbgewachs, MFM). Insurer Gutachten tend to set Wertminderung significantly lower.

**What does the Gutachten cost?**
In a no-fault accident the insurer pays — see [Sachverständigen-Kosten](/haftpflicht/sv-kosten).

**Is litigation worthwhile for a refused Wertminderung claim?**
Typically yes from a difference of €500. Legal costs are borne by the insurer in a no-fault accident, BGH case law on Wertminderung is clearly established — strong prospects for our partner law firm.
