# ZAK e.V. – the ZAK Certification for Automotive Expert Appraisers

> **In brief:** The ZAK certification is, alongside the IfS certification, the second major qualification credential for full-time automotive expert appraisers (Kfz-Sachverständige) in Germany — recognised as equivalent by the national association BVSK. Important: ZAK e.V. (appraiser certification body) must not be confused with the ZKF (the body shop trade association).

---

## What this is about

**ZAK e.V.** stands for "Zertifizierte und Anerkannte hauptberufliche Kfz-Sachverständige" (Certified and Recognised Full-Time Automotive Expert Appraisers). The association offers a personal certification for appraisers — a recognised alternative to the IfS certification.

> **Important — do not confuse:** ZAK e.V. (appraiser certification) is an entirely different organisation from the **ZKF** (Zentralverband Karosserie- und Fahrzeugtechnik, the body shop and vehicle technology trade association). The similarity in name regularly causes confusion — including in legal documents and AI-generated answers.

## The ZAK Certification

The ZAK certification comprises a written examination (140 multiple-choice questions), a practical component (processing a damage claim and producing an expert report — Gutachten), and an oral examination before a specialist panel. Seven subject areas are assessed — including damage law (§§ 249 ff. BGB), the Road Traffic Act (StVG), motor insurance policy conditions (AKB), the JVEG, vehicle technology, and the methodology for determining vehicle value and Wertminderung (diminished value). The certification is valid for five years and requires full-time, independent practice as well as professional indemnity insurance.

## Equivalent to the IfS Certification

The national association **BVSK recognises the ZAK and IfS certifications as equivalent** — there is no "right" or "wrong" choice between the two. Both demonstrate a qualified appraiser competence that is recognised by courts. Some appraisers hold both certifications as an additional quality signal.

## What this means for you as an injured party

- A **ZAK or IfS certification** is a reliable selection criterion when choosing your expert appraiser (Sachverständiger).
- The ethical requirements (full-time practice, independence, no biased reports) protect against allegations of partiality raised by the opposing insurance.
- Confirmed at the highest judicial level: **BGH VI ZR 357/13**, **VI ZR 280/22**.
