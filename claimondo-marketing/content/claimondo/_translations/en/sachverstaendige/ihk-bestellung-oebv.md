# Publicly Appointed and Sworn Expert (öbV) – The IHK Appointment

> **In brief:** A publicly appointed and sworn expert (öbV) is appointed under § 36 GewO by the IHK (German Chamber of Commerce and Industry) — the requirements are "significantly above-average specialist knowledge", personal suitability, and a sworn oath. The öbV appointment is an official state distinction and a premium quality signal with particularly high acceptance by courts and insurers.

---

## What This Is About

While IfS and ZAK certifications are issued by private associations, the **public appointment and sworn oath (öbV)** is an **official state** distinction: an appointing body — typically the Industrie- und Handelskammer (IHK) in the automotive sector — appoints the expert under **§ 36 GewO** for a specific subject area such as "vehicle damage and valuation."

## Requirements and Procedure

The core requirement is proof of **"significantly above-average specialist knowledge"** — assessed by an IHK expert panel through written, practical, and oral examination. Additional requirements include personal suitability (sound financial circumstances, no relevant entry in the Bundeszentralregister (federal criminal register)), professional indemnity insurance, and as a rule full-time professional activity. Upon passing, the expert is sworn in and entered into the nationwide directory **svv.ihk.de**. The appointment is valid for five years and can be renewed.

## Why This Matters for You as an Injured Party

- The öbV title is a **premium quality signal** — officially appointed by the state and sworn to neutrality.
- öbV expert reports (Gutachten) enjoy **particularly high acceptance** by courts and insurers; reductions are harder to enforce against them.
- That said: IfS- or ZAK-certified independent experts are also fully qualified — an öbV is not strictly required.

## What This Means for You as an Injured Party

- For disputed or high-value claims, an **öbV** can provide additional enforcement weight.
- Your **free right of choice** under § 249 BGB applies regardless.
- Backed by the highest court: **BGH VI ZR 67/06**, **VI ZR 357/13**, **VI ZR 280/22**; the selection of court-appointed experts is governed by §§ 404, 406 ZPO.
