# BVSK – Bundesverband der freien Kfz-Sachverständigen

> **In brief:** With over 1,100 members, the BVSK is Germany's largest association of independent Kfz-Sachverständigen (automotive appraisers). Its most important instrument is the BVSK-Honorarbefragung (fee survey), which the Bundesgerichtshof (Federal Court of Justice) recognises as a valid basis for estimating expert fees under § 287 ZPO (BGH VI ZR 357/13). A fee calculated according to the BVSK-Honorartabelle is in line with market rates and must be reimbursed in full by the opposing liability insurer.

---

## What this is about

The BVSK (Bundesverband der freiberuflichen und unabhängigen Sachverständigen für das Kraftfahrzeugwesen e.V.) represents independent, privately run appraisal offices — as distinct from large testing organisations such as DEKRA, GTÜ, KÜS or TÜV. With over 1,100 members, it is the market leader among free-market Kfz-Sachverständige and a recognised voice in dealings with the Gesamtverband der Deutschen Versicherungswirtschaft (GDV — German Insurance Association).

As an injured party (Geschädigter) who was not at fault, the BVSK is relevant to you primarily because its standards on fees, Wertminderung (diminished value), and adjacent-panel repainting (Beilackierung) are recognised by the courts and can be used as arguments against insurer reductions.

## The BVSK-Honorarbefragung (BGH-recognised)

The BVSK's most important instrument is the **BVSK-Honorarbefragung** (fee survey), conducted every two years (most recently 2024; next edition expected 2026). It records market-standard expert fees by damage-value bracket and has been recognised at the highest judicial level:

- **BGH VI ZR 357/13** — the BVSK-Honorarbefragung is a valid basis for estimating expert costs under § 287 ZPO.
- **BGH VI ZR 67/06** — the subject-based approach to damage assessment is authoritative; flat-rate ancillary costs are permissible, and the JVEG (statutory fee schedule for court-appointed experts) does not apply to privately instructed experts.
- **BGH VI ZR 225/13** — an expert invoice that has already been paid carries evidential weight for the necessity of those costs.

In practice this means: if your appraiser invoices according to the BVSK-Honorartabelle, that fee is in line with market rates and must be reimbursed by the opposing liability insurer.

## The BVSK Wertminderung model

The **BVSK-Wertminderungsmodell** is an established method for calculating merkantile Wertminderung (diminished market value). It takes into account the Wiederbeschaffungswert (replacement value), the extent of the damage, and correction factors for make, age, mileage and market demand. It stands on equal footing with other recognised methods (such as the MFM method) — within the framework of free damage estimation under § 287 ZPO, it is an accepted basis.

## Beilackierung (adjacent-panel repainting)

Through its Beilackierungsrichtlinie (repainting guideline), the BVSK takes the position that repainting adjacent panels is a recognised repair necessity when working with modern paints (multi-layer, metallic, pearlescent) — the majority of modern paint repairs require it. Insurers frequently strike this item; however, the Bundesgerichtshof has confirmed it as a reimbursable component of the damage (**BGH VI ZR 174/24**).

## Bagatellgrenze (minor-damage threshold)

As a rule of thumb, a Bagatellgrenze of approximately €715 applies: only from this damage level onwards is instructing an appraiser customary and economically justified; below it, a workshop estimate generally suffices. Courts of first instance draw the line differently — frequently between €750 and €1,000.

## What this means for you as an injured party

- **BVSK membership** of an appraiser is an indicator of quality.
- A **fee according to the BVSK-Honorartabelle** is market-standard and fully reimbursable (BGH case law).
- In the event of **insurer reductions**, BVSK standards serve as a well-founded counter-argument.

## Typical insurer objections — and what speaks against them

| Insurer objection | What speaks against it |
|---|---|
| "The BVSK fee is excessive." | The BVSK-Honorarbefragung is a BGH-recognised estimation basis (VI ZR 357/13). |
| "We calculate according to our own fee schedule." | § 287 ZPO permits the BVSK-Honorartabelle as an estimation basis. |
| "The Beilackierung was not necessary." | BGH VI ZR 174/24 + BVSK-Beilackierungsrichtlinie. |
| "We calculate Wertminderung differently." | The BVSK model is a permissible competing method (§ 287 ZPO). |
| "A photo assessment would have sufficed." | Above the Bagatellgrenze (~€715), a full Gutachten (expert report) is justified. |
