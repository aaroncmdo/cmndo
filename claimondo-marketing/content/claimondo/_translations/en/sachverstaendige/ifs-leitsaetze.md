# IfS and the IfS Guidelines – the Standard for Automotive Expert Reports

> **In brief:** The Institut für Sachverständigenwesen (IfS) in Köln is one of the central certification and standard-setting institutions for expert appraisers in Germany. Its "IfS-Leitsätze 2018 – Kraftfahrzeugschäden und -bewertung" (IfS Guidelines 2018 – Motor Vehicle Damage and Valuation) are the authoritative reference work for automotive expert reports (Gutachten) and are used equally by major inspection organisations, independent appraisers, and courts.

---

## What this is about

The **IfS (Institut für Sachverständigenwesen e.V.)** based in Köln is one of the leading institutions for the certification of automotive expert appraisers (Kfz-Sachverständige) and the publisher of the industry's authoritative guidelines. IfS certification is issued under **DIN EN ISO/IEC 17024** and is a strong quality signal — recognised by the national association BVSK as equivalent to ZAK certification.

## The IfS Guidelines 2018

The **IfS-Leitsätze "Kraftfahrzeugschäden und -bewertung"** (4th edition, 2018) are the standard reference work for producing expert reports (Gutachten) in Germany. Not only independent appraisers but also the major inspection organisations (DEKRA, GTÜ, KÜS, TÜV) and the courts follow them. They set out, among other things:

- the mandatory elements of a damage report (measurements, photographic documentation, plausibility checks, repair cost calculation, value assessment),
- the recognised methods for calculating diminished value (Wertminderung),
- the treatment of pre-existing and prior damage.

Because the guidelines are recognised across all industry associations, citing them is a strong argument whenever an insurer challenges the methodology of an expert report.

## IfS Certification as a Quality Signal

IfS certification requires a relevant professional qualification (engineer, technician, or master craftsperson), several years of experience, a formal examination, and professional indemnity insurance. Certification is valid for five years and must be renewed thereafter. For you as an injured party (Geschädigter), it is a reliable indicator of a qualified appraiser.

## What this means for you as an injured party

- **IfS certification** (or the equivalent ZAK) is a sound selection criterion when choosing your appraiser.
- In disputes over methodology, the **IfS-Leitsätze** serve as a cross-association benchmark.
- Confirmed at the highest judicial level: **BGH VI ZR 67/06** (subjective damage assessment), **VI ZR 357/13** (§ 287 ZPO), **VI ZR 280/22** (appraiser risk).
