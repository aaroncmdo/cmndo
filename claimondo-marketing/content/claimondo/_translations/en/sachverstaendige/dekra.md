# DEKRA – Major Inspection Organisation and Motor Vehicle Appraisers

> **In brief:** DEKRA is Germany's largest vehicle inspection organisation and also produces damage and valuation reports. An important point for injured parties: DEKRA appraisers are frequently commissioned by insurers. Under §249 BGB, however, you have the right to freely choose your own independent appraiser — you are not bound by the DEKRA expert sent by the insurer.

---

## What this is about

DEKRA SE (founded 1925, headquartered in Stuttgart) is Europe's largest vehicle inspection organisation. In addition to the statutory vehicle inspection (Hauptuntersuchung), it produces damage and valuation reports, accident reconstructions, and classic car assessments. This makes it one of the largest providers of damage appraisals — and through extensive insurer contracts, it maintains a key-account relationship with the insurance industry.

This is precisely the most important point for you as an injured party: if the opposing insurer wants to send "their" DEKRA appraiser, that appraiser's assessment is **not binding** on you.

## Your right to freely choose an appraiser

In the case of an accident you did not cause, you are in control of the claims settlement process. Under §249 BGB, you are entitled to commission your **own independent appraiser** of your choice — the costs are borne by the opposing liability insurer. An appraiser commissioned by the insurer will naturally assess matters in the insurer's interest; your own appraiser documents your damages fully and impartially.

## The DEKRA Repair Labour Rate (DRS)

A DEKRA publication relevant to the claims context is the annually published **DEKRA Reparatur Stundensatz (DRS)** — a postcode-differentiated overview of locally customary workshop labour rates, which has risen considerably in recent years. It serves as a reference figure for typical local rates, but is a **lower reference point, not an upper limit**: where there is an entitlement to a manufacturer-authorised workshop, higher labour rates are recoverable (**BGH VI ZR 91/09**).

## Qualifications and standards

DEKRA appraisers work within accredited structures in accordance with **DIN EN ISO/IEC 17020** (inspection bodies) and **17024** (personnel certification) — standards recognised by courts as quality indicators. DEKRA does not publish its own public methodology standard for appraisals; in practice, the industry aligns with the IfS guidelines (Leitsätze).

## What this means for you as an injured party

- DEKRA appraisers may be technically qualified — but when commissioned by an insurer, they are **structurally aligned with the insurer's side**.
- Your **free right of choice** under §249 BGB always applies: you may appoint your own independent appraiser, and the costs are borne by the opposing insurer.
- The DRS is a **comparative benchmark** for labour rates, not the final word.

## Typical insurer objections — and what speaks against them

| Insurer's objection | What speaks against it |
|---|---|
| "Our DEKRA appraiser is neutral." | Under §249 BGB, you have the right to your own independent appraiser. |
| "The DRS average is sufficient as a labour rate." | Where there is an entitlement to a manufacturer-authorised workshop, higher rates are recoverable (BGH VI ZR 91/09). |
| "DEKRA finds no Wertminderung (diminished value)." | What counts is the report produced by your own appraiser. |
