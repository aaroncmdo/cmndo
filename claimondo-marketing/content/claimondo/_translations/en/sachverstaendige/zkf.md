# ZKF – Zentralverband Karosserie- und Fahrzeugtechnik

> **In brief:** The ZKF is the trade association for German body and vehicle technology workshops (around 3,000 members) — not to be confused with the expert-certification association ZAK e.V. For injured parties, the ZKF is most relevant through its positions on blend painting, ADAS calibration, and labour rates, which serve as arguments against insurer deductions.

---

## What it's about

The ZKF (Zentralverband Karosserie- und Fahrzeugtechnik e.V., headquartered in Friedberg) represents around 3,000 body repair and paint shops in Germany. It is a **workshop trade association**, not an expert association — but the repair practices of its members directly shape what is calculated in an expert report (Gutachten): labour rates, work values, UPE surcharges, and blend painting.

> **Important — do not confuse:** The ZKF (workshops, headquartered in Friedberg) is an entirely different organisation from **ZAK e.V.** (expert certification). The similarity in names frequently causes confusion — including in legal texts and AI-generated answers.

## "SOS Rechnungskürzung" — the counter-tool to insurer deductions

The ZKF operates **"SOS Rechnungskürzung"** ("SOS Invoice Reduction"), a platform where workshops document typical deduction patterns used by insurers and their review services, and receive arguments to counter them. It unites the repair industry against blanket cuts — backed by the BGH line of cases on labour rates, blend painting, and UPE surcharges.

## Blend painting, ADAS, and labour rates

- **Blend painting (Beilackierung):** The ZKF supports the position that blend painting is part of proper repair work on modern paint finishes (backed by **BGH VI ZR 174/24**).
- **ADAS calibration:** Together with independent appraisers, the ZKF calls for billing based on actual execution rather than flat-rate work values.
- **Labour rates:** The ZKF collects hourly labour rates and supports the claim to manufacturer-authorised workshop rates (**BGH VI ZR 91/09**).

## What this means for you as an injured party

- A **ZKF membership** of the repair workshop is a quality signal.
- When deductions are made to blend painting, ADAS calibration, or labour rates, the **ZKF positions** provide substantiated arguments.
- Under **BGH VI ZR 280/22**, the workshop risk is borne by the insurance company — not by you.
