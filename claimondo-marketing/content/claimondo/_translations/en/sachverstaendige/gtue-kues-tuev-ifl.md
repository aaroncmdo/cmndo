# GTÜ, KÜS, TÜV and IFL e.V. – Major Inspection Organisations in Damage Assessment

> **In brief:** Alongside DEKRA, there are other major inspection organisations — GTÜ, KÜS and the TÜV companies — that also produce damage reports (Gutachten). None publish their own assessment standards; all follow the IfS guidelines. The shared industry body IFL e.V. provides, through its position papers on blending (Beilackierung) and ADAS calibration, important arguments against insurer deductions.

---

## What this is about

DEKRA is not the only major inspection organisation. Alongside it stand **GTÜ** (Gesellschaft für Technische Überwachung, founded 1977), **KÜS** (Kraftfahrzeug-Überwachungsorganisation freiberuflicher Kfz-Sachverständiger, founded 1989) and the **TÜV companies** (TÜV Süd, Nord, Rheinland, Thüringen). All focus primarily on vehicle roadworthiness inspections (Hauptuntersuchung); damage and valuation reports are offered as additional services — to a substantial degree at the TÜV companies and GTÜ.

As the injured party (Geschädigter), the same principle applies here: you are not required to accept an appraiser proposed by the insurer. Under **§ 249 BGB**, you freely choose your own independent expert (Sachverständiger).

## No proprietary assessment standard — the IfS guidelines as the benchmark

Neither GTÜ, nor KÜS, nor the TÜV companies publish their own comprehensive methodology manual for damage reports. All follow the **IfS guidelines** (Leitsätze) as the industry standard and operate within accredited structures under DIN EN ISO/IEC 17020/17024. The quality of a report therefore depends less on the organisation and more on the qualifications of the individual expert.

## IFL e.V. — the shared industry body

**IFL e.V.** (founded 2007) is the cross-organisational body in which independent experts, repair shops and inspection organisations jointly establish industry standards. Two of its positions are particularly valuable for injured parties, as these are precisely the areas where insurers tend to make deductions:

- **Paint blending (Beilackierung):** With modern paint systems, blending of adjacent panels is technically necessary in the majority of repairs — the Bundesgerichtshof (BGH) has confirmed it is reimbursable (**BGH VI ZR 174/24**).
- **ADAS calibration:** Calibration of advanced driver assistance systems is a mandatory part of the repair process and must be billed on a work-performed basis — flat-rate labour values are not permissible.

These position papers carry significant weight in disputes with insurers, as they are supported across all member organisations.

## What this means for you as the injured party

- GTÜ, KÜS and TÜV appraisers can be highly qualified — your **free right of choice** under § 249 BGB remains fully intact.
- Focus less on the organisation and more on **qualifications** (BVSK membership, IfS or ZAK certification).
- If deductions are made for paint blending (Beilackierung) or ADAS calibration, the **IFL position papers** are your argument.
