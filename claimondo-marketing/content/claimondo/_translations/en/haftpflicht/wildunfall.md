# Wildlife Accident – Who Pays, What to Do

> **Quick summary:** A wildlife accident (collision with a deer, wild boar, stag, etc.) is **not a liability claim** – there is no at-fault party in the traditional sense. Damage is covered by your **own partial comprehensive (Teilkasko)** or **fully comprehensive (Vollkasko) insurance**, provided you have taken out such a policy. Requirement: **wildlife accident certificate** (Wildunfall-Bescheinigung) issued by the police or the hunting leaseholder (Jagdpächter). The "force majeure" exclusion under § 7 Abs. 2 StVG applies in exceptional cases – typically, however, this is a straightforward own-insurance comprehensive claim.

## Also Known As

Wildlife damage · Wildlife collision · Deer accident · Wild boar accident

## Who Pays What

| Situation | Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Teilkasko** (partial comprehensive) in place | Material damage reimbursed, minus excess |
| **Vollkasko** (fully comprehensive) in place | Same as Teilkasko + potentially more |
| **Third-party liability only** | No entitlement – the injured party bears the cost |
| Wildlife accident caused by escaped domestic animals (dog, horse) | Owner liable under § 833 BGB |

## What to Do After a Wildlife Accident

1. **Stop and secure the scene** (hazard lights, warning triangle)
2. **Notify the police or Jagdpächter** – they issue the **Wildunfall-Bescheinigung**
3. **Document with photos** – animal and damage
4. **Never** take the animal with you or bury it (criminal offence: poaching)
5. **Notify your own insurer** promptly
6. **Commission a workshop** with an expert report (Gutachten) for damage from approx. €1,500

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does third-party liability cover a wildlife accident?**
No. A wildlife accident is classically an own-insurance comprehensive claim – your own Teilkasko or Vollkasko pays.

**What if I only have third-party liability insurance?**
You bear the cost yourself. Exception: demonstrable third-party causation (e.g. an escaped domestic animal belonging to a keeper).

**Do I need a Wildunfall-Bescheinigung?**
Yes. The police or Jagdpächter issue it – it is a prerequisite for comprehensive insurance reimbursement.

**Who is liable for an escaped horse or dog?**
The animal's keeper, under § 833 BGB. The claim is directed at the keeper and their liability insurance.
