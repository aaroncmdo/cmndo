# Finding the Opposing Insurance – How to Identify It Using the License Plate

> **Quick summary:** If you do not know which insurer covers the at-fault party's liability after an accident that was not your fault, you do not need to ask them directly. The **Zentralruf der Autoversicherer** (Central Call Center of Motor Insurers) identifies the responsible Kfz-Haftpflichtversicherung (motor liability insurance) for you — **free of charge**, at **0800 250 260 0**, around the clock, 365 days a year. All you need are three pieces of information: **license plate, date of loss, and country where the accident occurred**.

## Also known as

Zentralruf der Autoversicherer · identifying insurance by license plate · finding the at-fault party's liability insurer · insurer lookup

---

## In one sentence

The Zentralruf is a central information service run by German motor insurers that identifies the responsible Kfz-Haftpflichtversicherung from a license plate number — without you ever having to ask the at-fault party.

## The three pieces of information you need

Nothing more is required:

| Information | Example | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| Opposing license plate | K-AB 1234 | Photo taken at the scene, police report |
| Date of loss | 12.08.2026 | Your recollection, accident report |
| Country of accident | Germany | — |

Taking a photo of the license plate at the scene is therefore the single most important action after an accident — more important than exchanging phone numbers.

## How to reach the Zentralruf

**By phone from Germany:** **0800 250 260 0** — free of charge, **365 days a year, 24 hours a day**.

**From abroad:** +49 40 300 330 300, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.

**Online:** Via the request form at [zentralruf.de](https://www.zentralruf.de/) — your email address is also required there, and the information will be sent to it.

Both options are free of charge. You do not need to be a member or customer of any particular insurer.

## Why you do not need to ask the at-fault party

After an accident, the Verursacher (at-fault party) is legally required to provide their details. In practice, this frequently fails: the note is illegible, the insurer named turns out to be wrong, or contact breaks off once the question of fault becomes uncomfortable.

The Zentralruf bypasses this entirely. It queries the insurers' central database — not the at-fault party. This makes you completely independent of their cooperation.

## If you do not have the license plate

Without a license plate, the Zentralruf cannot help — that is a different situation: the Verursacher is unknown, meaning **Fahrerflucht** (hit-and-run). In that case, **Verkehrsopferhilfe e.V.** steps in under § 12 PflVG when the at-fault party is unknown (property damage with a €500 excess, personal injury claims in full). A police report is among the prerequisites.

→ Full details on the [Fahrerflucht](/haftpflicht/fahrerflucht) page.

## What happens next — sequence matters

Once you know the insurer, the rule is: **Gutachten (expert report) first, then notification.** Anyone who reports the claim before an independent Sachverständiger (expert appraiser) has inspected the vehicle hands control over the damage assessment to the opposing side — who will then send their own audit service.

As an injured party who was not at fault, you have the right under § 249 BGB to commission your **own** Sachverständiger. The costs are borne by the opposing insurance.

→ Continue with [Who is entitled to claim](/haftpflicht/geschaedigte-primaer) and the [4-week deadline](/haftpflicht/4-wochen-frist).

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the information really free of charge?**
Yes. The Zentralruf is free to call on the 0800 number, and the online request is also free.

**Do I receive the information immediately?**
By phone, usually in the course of the call itself. For online requests, the answer is sent to the email address you provide.

**What if the at-fault party gave me the wrong insurer?**
The Zentralruf's information takes precedence. It comes directly from the insurers' own database, not from an accident participant's recollection.

**Does the Zentralruf also help with accidents abroad?**
The country of accident is one of the mandatory details — so the lookup is not limited to Germany. From abroad, use +49 40 300 330 300 (Mon–Fri, 8 a.m.–8 p.m.).
