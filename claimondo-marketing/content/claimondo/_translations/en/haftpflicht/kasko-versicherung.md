# Comprehensive vs. Third-Party Liability Insurance – When to Use Which

> **In brief:** **Your own Kaskoversicherung (comprehensive insurance)** – partial (Teilkasko) or full (Vollkasko) – covers your own vehicle regardless of fault, but **affects your SF-Klasse (no-claims discount class)** (downgrade). **The at-fault party's liability insurance** pays in full for accidents you did not cause, with no impact on your SF-Klasse. Rule of thumb: if the other party is clearly at fault, always use the **direct claim against their liability insurer** under **§ 115 VVG** – never your own Kaskoversicherung. **Quasi-Deckung (interim cover)**: your Kaskoversicherung pays first, then recovers the money from the at-fault insurer via subrogation (§ 86 VVG analogously).

## Also Known As

Eigene Kasko · Vollkasko · Teilkasko · Quasi-Deckung · SF-Klassen-Rückstufung

## Who Pays in Each Situation

| Situation | Who pays |
|---|---|
| **Accident caused by the other party, clear fault established** | At-fault party's liability insurer (§ 115 VVG) – in full, no SF-Klasse impact |
| **Your own fault** | Vollkasko (with excess + downgrade) |
| **Wildlife collision** | Teilkasko |
| **Theft, fire, hail** | Teilkasko |
| **Vandalism, glass damage** | Teilkasko or Vollkasko |
| **Intentional damage by a third party** | Vollkasko + subrogation |
| **Hit-and-run (property damage)** | Vollkasko + Verkehrsopferhilfe (motor vehicle victim compensation scheme) |

## Teilkasko vs. Vollkasko

| Coverage area | Teilkasko | Vollkasko |
|---|---|---|
| Wildlife collision, fire, theft | ✓ | ✓ |
| Hail, storm | ✓ | ✓ |
| Glass damage | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Own fault** | ✗ | ✓ |
| **Vandalism** | ✗ | ✓ |
| Single-vehicle accident (e.g. hitting a tree) | ✗ | ✓ |
| SF-Klasse impact on claim | No | Yes |
| Excess | yes (often €150) | yes (often €300–€500) |

## Important: Quasi-Deckung (Interim Cover)

If the at-fault party's insurer delays payment or rejects the claim, your Kaskoversicherung can settle the claim upfront (**Quasi-Deckung**) – it then recovers the funds from the opposing insurer via subrogation. Advantage: fast payout. Disadvantage: SF-Klasse downgrade – which is **reversed** upon successful subrogation recovery (established BGH case law).

## SF-Klassen-Rückstufung (No-Claims Discount Downgrade)

When you claim on your Kaskoversicherung:
- A Vollkasko claim results in an SF-Klasse downgrade (typically 3–7 classes)
- This leads to increased premiums over several years – typically €3,000–€8,000 in additional costs
- With Quasi-Deckung: the downgrade is reversed following successful subrogation

→ **Rule of thumb**: If the other party is clearly at fault, **never** use your own Kaskoversicherung. Engage a lawyer and enforce the direct claim against the opposing insurer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Should I use my own Kaskoversicherung?**
Only if you are at fault, or for losses specific to your Kaskoversicherung (wildlife, fire, theft). If the other party is clearly at fault: use their liability insurer – no SF-Klasse impact.

**What is Quasi-Deckung?**
Your Kaskoversicherung pays first and recovers the money from the at-fault insurer later. This provides fast liquidity but carries the risk of an SF-Klasse downgrade in the interim.

**When is the SF-Klasse reinstated?**
Following successful subrogation recovery from the opposing insurer – your Vollkasko restores your SF-Klasse accordingly.

**Is Vollkasko worth it for a new vehicle?**
For new vehicles up to 5 years old and up to 80,000 km, typically yes. Beyond that, a cost-benefit calculation is required – often not worthwhile for older vehicles.
