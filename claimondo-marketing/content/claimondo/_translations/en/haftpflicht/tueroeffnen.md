# Car Door Accident – Who Is Liable When a Door Opens Into Moving Traffic

> **Quick summary:** Anyone who opens a vehicle door into moving traffic and causes an accident bears **full liability (100 : 0)** under § 14 StVO (German Road Traffic Regulations) and the prima facie evidence rule (Anscheinsbeweis) — regardless of whether the other road user is a car, lorry, cyclist, or motorcyclist. Even a momentary lapse is sufficient to establish fault. In so-called **"dooring"** incidents (opening a door in front of a cyclist), particularly strict duties of care apply — the split is typically 100 : 0 against the person opening the door.

## Also Known As

§ 14 StVO Türöffnen · Dooring · Tür-Kollision

## Fault Split by Scenario

| Scenario | Door opener : Other party |
|---|---|
| Door opened without checking, car collides | **100 : 0** |
| Door opened, cyclist collides (dooring) | **100 : 0** |
| Door opened + other vehicle significantly speeding | 80 : 20 |
| Passenger opens door without checking | 100 : 0 (occupant responsibility) |

## Legal Framework

§ 14 Abs. 1 StVO: *"Anyone getting in or out must behave in such a way that other road users are not endangered."*

**Consequence:** The strictest duty of care applies when opening a vehicle door — a shoulder check and mirror check are mandatory. The Anscheinsbeweis (prima facie evidence rule) operates against the person who opened the door.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is liable in a car door accident?**
The person who opened the door — Anscheinsbeweis applies, standard split 100 : 0.

**What about dooring (door opened in front of a cyclist)?**
Full liability falls on the person who opened the door. Where the cyclist suffers personal injury, courts frequently award increased Schmerzensgeld (pain and suffering damages) on account of its punitive function (Genugtuungs-Funktion).

**What if the passing vehicle was speeding?**
Where there is a significant speed excess, a contributory fault share of 20 % may apply. At normal speed, the injured party is fully protected.
