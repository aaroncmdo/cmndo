# Prima Facie Evidence in Traffic Accidents – When It Works in Your Favour

> **In brief:** The Anscheinsbeweis (prima facie evidence) is an evidentiary relief mechanism in civil proceedings. In standard constellations — rear-end collisions, right-of-way violations, red-light infractions — the first appearance of the facts points to typical fault on the part of one party. You are required to prove **nothing**; the opposing party must rebut the prima facie evidence with concrete facts. In many traffic accident constellations, the Anscheinsbeweis is your most important procedural advantage.

## Also Known As

Prima facie evidence · Beweis des ersten Anscheins (proof by first appearance) · Typischer Geschehensablauf (typical sequence of events)

---

## Explained in One Sentence

The Anscheinsbeweis is an evidentiary relief mechanism by which the court infers typical fault from a typical sequence of events — shifting the burden of proof to the opposing party.

## Explained in Three Sentences

In clearly typified traffic accident constellations (rear-end collisions, right-of-way violations, red-light infractions, lane-change collisions), case law has established the Anscheinsbeweis — the external sequence of events implies a specific degree of fault so strongly that no further evidence from the injured party is required. The opposing party can only rebut the Anscheinsbeweis by presenting **concrete facts** that suggest an **atypical** sequence of events — vague assertions or general possibilities are not sufficient. In practice, this means: in the classic constellations, courts regularly rule **100% against the typical at-fault party**, without the need for extensive evidentiary proceedings.

## The Five Key Figures

- **100% to 0%** — typical apportionment in rear-end collisions (Anscheinsbeweis applies)
- **100% to 0%** — typical apportionment in right-of-way violations
- **100% to 0%** — typical apportionment in red-light infractions
- **70–100%** against the lane-changer in lane-change collisions
- **0%** evidentiary burden on you — the opposing party must rebut

---

## What Anscheinsbeweis Means — Legally

In civil proceedings, the basic rule applies: whoever asserts a claim must prove all facts establishing that claim ([Beweislast / burden of proof](/haftpflicht/beweislast)). In traffic accidents, this would often be difficult — you would need to document in minute detail who performed which manoeuvre at what speed and at what point in time.

Case law has therefore developed the Anscheinsbeweis for **typical sequences of events**: once the external facts are established, the court infers a specific cause **by reference to rules of experience**. Example:

- **Facts:** Vehicle A rear-ends vehicle B, which was stationary or driving normally.
- **Prima facie inference:** A maintained insufficient following distance or was not paying attention.
- **Legal consequence:** A is at fault — without further evidentiary proceedings.

The opposing party can only **rebut** the Anscheinsbeweis by presenting **concrete facts** suggesting an atypical sequence (e.g., "B braked suddenly and without cause on the motorway"). A mere possibility is not sufficient — facts must be pleaded and proven.

## The Most Important Anscheinsbeweis Constellations in Traffic Law

### 1. Rear-End Collision
**Prima facie inference:** The rear driver failed to maintain sufficient following distance or was not paying attention (§ 4 StVO).
**Apportionment:** Typically 100% against the rear driver.
**Rebuttal:** Sudden and causeless braking by the vehicle in front, mechanical defect, external interference. **Proof required** — mere assertion is not sufficient.

### 2. Right-of-Way Violation
**Prima facie inference:** Whoever fails to yield (§ 8 StVO — "right before left", priority road) bears primary fault.
**Apportionment:** 100% against the party who violated the right of way.
**Rebuttal:** The party with right of way was travelling significantly too fast or was not paying attention — concrete evidence required.

### 3. Red-Light Infraction
**Prima facie inference:** Whoever drives through a red light bears the fault.
**Apportionment:** 100% against the red-light offender, often even where the opposing party contributed.
**Rebuttal:** Sudden mechanical defect, vis maior (force majeure), cross-traffic violation as a temporally synchronous fact — difficult to establish.

### 4. Lane Change (§ 7 StVO)
**Prima facie inference:** Whoever changes lanes without regard for straight-travelling traffic is primarily at fault.
**Apportionment:** 70–100% against the lane-changer, depending on the constellation.
**Rebuttal:** The straight-travelling driver was significantly exceeding the speed limit or was travelling in a lane that was barely usable due to a traffic obstruction.

### 5. Reversing / Pulling Out of a Parking Space
**Prima facie inference:** Whoever reverses or pulls out of a parking space bears a heightened duty of care (§ 9 para. 5 StVO).
**Apportionment:** Typically 100% against the reversing driver.

### 6. Opening a Vehicle Door
**Prima facie inference:** Whoever opens a door into flowing traffic (§ 14 StVO) bears the fault.
**Apportionment:** 100% against the person opening the door.

### 7. Turning Around on the Road
**Prima facie inference:** Whoever performs a U-turn bears a heightened duty of care (§ 9 para. 5 StVO).
**Apportionment:** 100% against the turning driver.

## What This Means Concretely for Your Claim Settlement

### Advantage 1 — No Burden of Proof on Your Part
In Anscheinsbeweis constellations, you are **not** required to prove the opposing party's fault. It is sufficient to establish the external facts — for example, via a police report or witness statements confirming that it was a rear-end collision.

### Advantage 2 — The Insurer Must Rebut
The opposing insurer can only impose blanket contributory negligence apportionments if it rebuts the Anscheinsbeweis with **concrete facts**. Mere assertions are legally insufficient.

### Advantage 3 — Quick Win in Litigation
In clear Anscheinsbeweis constellations, courts frequently rule in your favour **without evidentiary proceedings** — litigation is short, the probability of success is high.

## What the Insurance Company Typically Claims

> *"The question of fault is not clear-cut — we are apportioning liability 70:30."*

In an Anscheinsbeweis constellation: not acceptable. What **specific fact** is supposed to rebut the Anscheinsbeweis? Blanket apportionments are legally impermissible.

> *"Our policyholder has a different perception of events."*

A party's own perception is not a rebutting fact. There must be objectively verifiable facts.

> *"We are able to rebut the prima facie inference."*

The appropriate response: With what, specifically? Which evidence, which facts? A blanket assertion is legally insufficient.

## What You Should Do Now — Concretely

### 💡 The Most Important Rule
The Anscheinsbeweis applies **automatically** in your favour in standard constellations. You do not need to "fight" — you need to present the constellation clearly.

### 🛠 Three Steps

**1. Document the facts thoroughly**
Police report, witness statements, accident sketch, photographs. The clearer the external facts, the clearer the Anscheinsbeweis.

**2. Name the Anscheinsbeweis explicitly**
In correspondence with the insurer, state in precise terms: "The Anscheinsbeweis applies on the basis of [constellation, e.g. rear-end collision]." This sets the legal anchor point.

**3. Reject rebuttal attempts**
When the insurer argues against the Anscheinsbeweis: demand concrete facts and concrete evidence. Reject blanket assertions in writing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the Anscheinsbeweis?**
An evidentiary relief mechanism in civil proceedings: in typical sequences of events, the court infers a specific cause by reference to rules of experience. You are not required to prove anything — the opposing party must rebut with concrete facts.

**Who is at fault in a rear-end collision?**
As a rule, the rear driver — Anscheinsbeweis on the basis of failure to maintain following distance or inattention (§ 4 StVO). Typical apportionment: 100% against the rear driver.

**Do I have to prove that I am not at fault?**
No. In Anscheinsbeweis constellations, the burden of proof lies with the opposing party. You only need to establish the external facts.

**How does one rebut an Anscheinsbeweis?**
With concrete facts that suggest an atypical sequence of events. A mere possibility or assertion is not sufficient — facts must be pleaded and, where necessary, proven.

**In which constellations does the Anscheinsbeweis not apply?**
In atypical, difficult-to-reconstruct constellations — for example, parking damage without witnesses, side-swipe collisions with an unclear traffic situation, right-of-way situations with mutual obstruction of view. In these cases, evidence must be established in the conventional manner.

**Is it worth engaging a lawyer when the Anscheinsbeweis applies in my favour?**
Yes — legal costs are paid by the opposing insurer (BGH VI ZR 235/13). Moreover, without a lawyer, insurers more frequently push through blanket contributory negligence apportionments that are legally untenable.
