# Multiple At-Fault Parties / Chain Collision – Joint and Several Liability under § 421 BGB

> **In brief:** When multiple vehicles are involved (chain collision, mass pile-up) and several registered keepers contributed to the damage, they are liable under § 421 BGB (German Civil Code) as **joint and several debtors** — every injured party can claim the **full** amount from **any** of the at-fault parties. The apportionment between the at-fault parties is handled in the **internal settlement** (§ 426 BGB) — that is not your concern. You choose whom to pursue first — typically the party with the clearest liability and the most identifiable insurance coverage.

## Also known as

§ 421 BGB · Gesamtschuldner · chain collision · mass pile-up

## What joint and several liability means

| Position | Injured party |
|---|---|
| Claims against multiple at-fault parties | In full (joint and several) |
| Right of choice | You choose which party to pursue |
| If one at-fault party makes a partial payment | The remainder can be claimed from the others |
| Internal settlement (§ 426 BGB) | Matter for the at-fault parties / their insurers |

## Typical scenarios

### Classic chain collision
- Vehicle A rear-ends B, pushing B into C
- C is the primary injured party and can pursue both A and B
- A and B settle internally (typically A bears primary fault)

### Mass pile-up
- Multiple independent rear-end / collision events
- Apportionment highly complex — legal representation essential
- Insurers settle among themselves

### Multiple at-fault parties in different configurations
- E.g. a driver failing to yield + a rear-end driver
- Apportionment between them under § 17 StVG

## Practical consequence

You typically pursue the **party with the strongest liability** (clearest fault, identifiable insurer):
- Their insurer settles your claim in full
- Their insurer recovers proportionate shares from the other at-fault parties via internal settlement
- You do not have to pursue multiple parties yourself

## Frequently asked questions

**Who is liable in a chain collision?**
All at-fault parties involved, as joint and several debtors under § 421 BGB. You claim from one; that party settles internally.

**What is joint and several liability?**
Multiple debtors are liable jointly, each individually for the full amount. The injured party chooses whom to pursue.

**What is the internal settlement?**
The apportionment among the debtors under § 426 BGB — determined by each party's contribution to the cause of the damage.

**Is it worth suing all parties?**
Rarely. The standard approach is to pursue one at-fault party — typically one who is clearly solvent and identifiable — who then recovers the proportionate shares from the others.
