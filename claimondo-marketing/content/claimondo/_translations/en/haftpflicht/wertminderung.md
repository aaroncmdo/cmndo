# Diminished Value After a Car Accident – Definition, Methods, Calculation

> **Quick summary:** Merkantile Wertminderung (diminished value) is the loss in resale value of a repaired vehicle on the used-car market — even after a fully correct repair — because the vehicle is now classified as an "accident car." It is compensated **in addition** to repair costs (§ 249 BGB) and is calculated by an independent expert (Sachverständiger) using recognised methods (Ruhkopf-Sahm, Halbgewachs-Höning, MFM, Berens-Hettberg-Strunk). Typical ranges: €200–€700 for moderate damage, €1,500–€5,000 for major damage.

## Also known as

Merkantile Wertminderung · Minderwert · Wertverlust nach Unfall · diminished value after accident

---

## Explained in one sentence

Wertminderung (diminished value) is the drop in your vehicle's resale value caused solely by the accident — even after repair — and constitutes an independent head of damages under § 249 BGB.

## Explained in three sentences

A vehicle declared an "accident car" achieves lower prices on the used-car market — this loss in value is the **merkantile Wertminderung** (diminished value), an independent head of damages under § 249 BGB recognised in consistent BGH case law. It is calculated by an independent Sachverständiger (expert appraiser) using one of the four recognised methods (Ruhkopf-Sahm, Halbgewachs-Höning, MFM — Modifizierte Frankfurter Methode, Berens-Hettberg-Strunk) — the choice of method must be justified in the Gutachten (expert report). The widely cited rule of thumb "no diminished value for vehicles over 5 years or 100,000 km" is **not a binding threshold**, but a guideline — individual assessment decides, especially for high-value vehicles.

## The five key figures

- **§ 249 BGB** as the legal basis for the claim
- **4 recognised methods** (Ruhkopf-Sahm, Halbgewachs-Höning, MFM, Berens-Hettberg-Strunk)
- **€1,500** typical minor-damage threshold
- **5 years / 100,000 km** orientation benchmark (not a hard rule!)
- **€200–€5,000** typical Wertminderung range depending on damage severity

---

## Requirements for a Wertminderung claim

Three conditions must be met (BGH line):

1. **Proper repair** — diminished value applies to repaired vehicles, not to total losses.
2. **Substantial damage** — above the minor-damage threshold (typically from €1,500 in repair costs).
3. **Loss of market acceptance** — a demonstrable drop in value on the used-car market.

---

## The four recognised calculation methods

### Ruhkopf-Sahm method (classic, widely used)
Formula-based: Wertminderung = (Wiederbeschaffungswert + repair costs) × factor depending on age, type of damage, and repair scope. Established since the 1960s and recognised by many courts.

### Halbgewachs-Höning method
Differentiated assessment covering severity of damage, structural component involvement, and repair quality. Places greater weight on the vehicle's market attractiveness.

### MFM – Modifizierte Frankfurter Methode
A more modern approach using concrete market analysis — comparing sale prices of accident vehicles with accident-free equivalent vehicles.

### Berens-Hettberg-Strunk method
Designed primarily for premium vehicles and more serious damage.

→ The Sachverständiger selects the method appropriate to the specific case. Insurers frequently reduce payments citing a "different method" — this is not permissible where the expert's methodological choice is properly justified.

---

## Typical Wertminderung ranges (guidance)

| Damage severity (repair costs) | Typical Wertminderung |
|---|---|
| Under €1,500 | usually €0 (minor-damage range) |
| €1,500 – €5,000 | €200 – €700 |
| €5,000 – €15,000 | €500 – €2,000 |
| €15,000 – €30,000 | €1,500 – €5,000 |
| Over €30,000 | up to €8,000+ |

→ Structural damage (longitudinal members, sills, A/B/C pillars) results in significantly higher Wertminderung than purely cosmetic bodywork damage. Premium brands (BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Porsche) typically come in 30–50% above average figures.

---

## Vehicle age and mileage — no fixed threshold

The widely cited rule of thumb "no Wertminderung for vehicles over 5 years or 100,000 km" is **not a BGH line**, but an older expert convention. In practice:

- **< 3 years / < 50,000 km:** Wertminderung typically high
- **3–5 years / 50,000–100,000 km:** Wertminderung possible
- **> 5 years / > 100,000 km:** harder to enforce, **but possible** — particularly for high-value models, well-maintained vehicles, and those with a complete authorised-dealer service history

The BGH rejects a rigid age threshold — individual assessment decides. See also [Decoder "Wertminderung nicht angefallen"](/decoder/wertminderung-nicht).

## What to do now — concretely

### 💡 The most important rule
Wertminderung appears in the Sachverständigen-Gutachten (expert report). Without an explicit figure in the report, it will **not** be compensated automatically — you must actively assert the claim.

### 🛠 Three steps

**1. Commission a Gutachten with Wertminderung calculation**
Engage a BVSK-certified Sachverständiger — Wertminderung must be explicitly quantified, with the method named.

**2. List Wertminderung separately in the claim letter**
Itemise repair costs and Wertminderung as distinct positions. Insurers often settle only the positions explicitly claimed.

**3. If refused: use the Decoder response letter**
See [Decoder "Wertminderung nicht angefallen"](/decoder/wertminderung-nicht).

## Frequently asked questions

**How is Wertminderung calculated?**
By a Sachverständiger using one of the four recognised methods (Ruhkopf-Sahm, Halbgewachs-Höning, MFM, Berens-Hettberg-Strunk). The chosen method must be justified in the Gutachten.

**Can I claim Wertminderung for a 7-year-old vehicle?**
Possibly. The rule of thumb "no Wertminderung from 5 years" is not a BGH line. For premium brands, well-maintained vehicles, and a complete authorised-dealer service history, Wertminderung can be claimed beyond 5 years.

**Is Wertminderung taxable?**
No. Wertminderung is damages compensation and is tax-free — as is Schmerzensgeld (pain and suffering compensation).

**Can I claim Wertminderung with a fictional (fiktive) settlement?**
Yes. Wertminderung is independent of whether you actually carry out the repair — it reflects the loss in market value.

**Can the insurer challenge the claim with their own expert report?**
Yes, but your own Sachverständigen-Gutachten remains an equally valid basis for estimation. In the event of a dispute, a court-appointed expert decides.

**Is it worth litigating over refused Wertminderung?**
Typically yes, for differences from €500 upwards. Legal costs are borne by the insurer, and the BGH line is clearly established.
