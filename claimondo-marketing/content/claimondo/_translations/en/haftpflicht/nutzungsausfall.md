# Loss of Use Compensation (Nutzungsausfall): Amount, Entitlement and Rate Table After an Accident

> **In brief:** If your vehicle is in for repairs or replacement after an accident that was not your fault and you **do not hire a rental car**, you are entitled to **flat-rate loss-of-use compensation** (Nutzungsausfall-Entschädigung, NAE) — recognised by the courts since BGH GSZ 1/86. Daily rates under the **Sanden-Danner-Tabelle** range from **€27** (small cars) to **€175 per day** (luxury class). No proof of specific journeys required — private vehicles are covered by the presumption-of-use doctrine.

## Also known as

NAE · Nutzungsausfallentschädigung · Flat-rate mobility compensation · Vehicle-deprivation loss

---

## Explained in one sentence

Nutzungsausfall (loss of use) is a flat-rate monetary payment for the period your vehicle was unavailable due to an accident — calculated using the Sanden-Danner-Tabelle and the actual repair duration.

## Explained in three sentences

Instead of hiring a rental car, you may claim flat-rate loss-of-use compensation (BGH GSZ 1/86) — it covers the loss resulting from being deprived of your vehicle, **without** you having to prove specific journeys or actual additional costs. The benchmark is the **Sanden-Danner-Tabelle**, with daily rates per vehicle class — typically €27 (small car) to €175 (luxury class), multiplied by the **actual downtime**. For privately used vehicles, the **presumption-of-use doctrine** applies: the insurer must prove you did not use the vehicle — not the other way around.

## The five key figures

- **BGH GSZ 1/86** — the landmark ruling
- **€27–175/day** depending on vehicle class (Sanden-Danner-Tabelle range)
- **14 days** — standard period for total loss cases
- **Presumption-of-use doctrine** — no proof of use required
- **§ 249 BGB** — the statutory basis

---

## Eligibility requirements

Three conditions must be met:

1. **Vehicle unavailability** due to an accident that was not your fault (repair or replacement)
2. **No rental car hired** (alternative: [rental car reimbursement](/haftpflicht/mietwagen))
3. **Intended use** — you planned to use the vehicle during the period in question

→ A rental car AND Nutzungsausfall are **mutually exclusive** — you choose one option.

---

## Sanden-Danner-Tabelle — daily rates by vehicle class

The Sanden-Danner-Tabelle (Sanden/Danner/Küppersbusch) is the court-recognised standard for flat-rate estimation:

| Vehicle class | Examples | Daily rate (indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Class A – Small car | VW Polo, Renault Twingo | €27–32/day |
| Class B – Compact | VW Golf, Opel Astra | €35–45/day |
| Class C – Mid-range | VW Passat, Audi A4 | €45–65/day |
| Class D – Upper mid-range | BMW 5 Series, Mercedes E-Class | €65–95/day |
| Class E+ – Luxury | BMW 7 Series, Mercedes S-Class, Audi A8 | €95–175/day |
| SUV / Premium SUV | model-dependent | equivalent to C–E |

→ These figures are indicative. Exact rates follow the current Sanden-Danner-Tabelle. Special vehicle types (classic cars, premium saloons, motorhomes) have supplementary specific values.

## Presumption of use — you prove nothing

The BGH has consistently held: for privately used vehicles, **the intention and actual use of the vehicle are presumed**. In practice:

- **You do not need to prove specific journeys** — no fuel receipts, no logbook
- **A statutory declaration** confirming typical use is sufficient
- **The insurer must prove non-use** (e.g. an extended stay abroad) — which is difficult in practice

→ This presumption protects you against insurer arguments such as "not evidenced" — see [Decoder "Nutzungsausfall nicht erstattbar"](/decoder/nutzungsausfall-nicht).

## Second vehicle — what applies

A second vehicle in the household may reduce or exclude Nutzungsausfall — depending on the **specific household situation**:

| Situation | Effect on NAE |
|---|---|
| Two working adults, one vehicle each | Entitlement remains — second vehicle is otherwise needed |
| One person, two vehicles | Reduction or exclusion possible |
| Family second car used occasionally | Entitlement remains — main vehicle not substituted |

## Rental car or Nutzungsausfall — making the choice

| | Rental car | Nutzungsausfall |
|---|---|---|
| Requirement | Actual hire | Presumption of use |
| Amount | Actual daily rate | Sanden-Danner flat rate |
| Mobility | Available | None |
| Effort | Hiring and administration | None |
| Typical amount (mid-range, 14 days) | ~€700–1,000 | ~€770 |

→ Rule of thumb: if you can cover your mobility needs via a second vehicle, public transport, cycling or working from home, Nutzungsausfall is usually the better financial choice. If you need a vehicle, a rental car is the right option.

## What to do now — step by step

### 💡 The most important rule
Nutzungsausfall is a standalone claim item. If you do not hire a rental car, **always** claim Nutzungsausfall — do not overlook it. The insurer will wait to see whether you ask.

### 🛠 Three steps

**1. Identify your vehicle class**
If in doubt: the expert report (Gutachten) will specify the class.

**2. Document the repair duration**
Workshop order, handback date, any parts-availability delays. For total loss: 14 days replacement duration as the BGH standard.

**3. Submit the claim in writing**
List Nutzungsausfall as a separate line item in your claim letter. State the exact amount (daily rate × duration).

## Frequently asked questions

**How much is Nutzungsausfall per day?**
Between €27 and €175 per day depending on vehicle class, per the Sanden-Danner-Tabelle. Mid-range vehicles typically fall at €45–65/day.

**Who determines my vehicle's class?**
The expert (Sachverständiger) in the Gutachten — or you can look it up by comparing your model against the Sanden-Danner-Tabelle. A BVSK-certified appraiser assigns the class as a matter of routine.

**Do I need to claim Nutzungsausfall actively, or is it paid automatically?**
You must claim it actively. Insurers frequently settle only the items explicitly requested — failing to mention Nutzungsausfall is one of the most common gaps.

**For how long is Nutzungsausfall paid?**
For the actual repair or replacement duration. In total loss cases, 14 days is the BGH standard.

**Can I receive both a rental car and Nutzungsausfall?**
No — the two are mutually exclusive. You choose one.

**What if I was on holiday during the downtime period?**
This may reduce your entitlement if the insurer can prove you were abroad. Mentioning a holiday in your initial damage report should be avoided.
