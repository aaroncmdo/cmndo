# Parking Lot Accidents – Who Is Liable on Private Property

> **In brief:** Parking lots are generally governed by **mutual consideration** (§ 1 StVO) – the right-of-way rule applies only where the lanes have **road character** (clearly marked driving lanes). When pulling out of a parking space, the **person reversing typically bears primary fault** (§ 10 StVO by analogy). Standard liability splits vary significantly by scenario: from 50 : 50 to 100 : 0.

## Also known as

Parking lot damage · Supermarket parking lot accident · Parking lot collision

## Liability split by scenario

| Scenario | Split |
|---|---|
| Reversing driver against through traffic in driving lane | **100 : 0** against reversing driver |
| Both drivers reversing (mutually) | 50 : 50 |
| Both moving in driving lane (equal right of way) | 50 : 50 |
| Driving lane with road character – right of way applies | see [Right of Way / Right Before Left](/haftpflicht/vorfahrt-rechts-vor-links) |
| While parking – other driver in driving lane | 80 : 20 against the parking driver |
| Door opening into parked vehicle – see [Door Opening](/haftpflicht/tueroeffnen) | 100 : 0 |

## What defines a parking lot in legal terms

**Parking lot with road character** = driving lanes clearly marked as traffic routes, similar to a public road → German Road Traffic Act (StVO) including right-of-way rules applies.

**Pure parking area** without road character = mutual consideration, no right-of-way scheme → § 1 StVO, proportional fault allocation.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does right-before-left apply in a parking lot?**
Only in driving lanes with road character. On pure parking areas: mutual consideration applies.

**Who is at fault when reversing out of a space?**
Typically the reversing driver – the prima facie evidence rule (Anscheinsbeweis) applies, standard split 100 : 0.

**What about door opening?**
See [Door Opening](/haftpflicht/tueroeffnen) – 100 : 0 against the person opening the door.
