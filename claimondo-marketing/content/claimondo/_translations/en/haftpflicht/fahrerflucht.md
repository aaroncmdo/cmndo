# Hit-and-Run Accidents – What Injured Parties Can Do

> **In brief:** If the at-fault driver flees the scene and cannot be identified, you as the injured party still have protection: **Verkehrsopferhilfe e.V.** (VOH) steps in under § 12 PflVG (German Compulsory Insurance Act) when the at-fault party remains unknown or is uninsured. Requirements: **file a police report** within seven days and provide demonstrable efforts to identify the perpetrator. Personal injury claims are covered in full; property damage claims carry an excess (€500 for property damage, none for personal injury).

## Also Known As

Hit-and-run · § 142 StGB · Fahrerflucht · Verkehrsopferhilfe

## What to Do Immediately

1. **Call the police (110)** – a police report is mandatory for VOH coverage to apply
2. **Preserve evidence** – photos, paint fragments, witness contact details, dashcam footage
3. **Notify your own insurer**
4. **Commission an expert report (Gutachten)** – see [expert costs](/haftpflicht/sv-kosten)
5. **Submit a Verkehrsopferhilfe application** within 7 days

## Verkehrsopferhilfe e.V. – the Safety-Net Solution

Operated by: Verband der Versicherungswirtschaft. Registered office: Hamburg.

**Covers** cases involving:
- An unknown at-fault party (hit-and-run)
- An uninsured at-fault party
- An insolvent insurer
- Vehicles used unlawfully and intentionally (theft, unauthorised use)

⚠ **Was the licence plate noted – for example by a witness?** In that case the at-fault party is not unknown, and VOH is the wrong route (the €500 excess applies, which you would otherwise avoid). You can identify the responsible liability insurer directly via the [Zentralruf der Autoversicherer](/haftpflicht/gegnerische-versicherung-ermitteln) – free of charge; the licence plate number, date of loss, and country where the accident occurred are sufficient.

**What is covered:**
- **Personal injury:** in full
- **Property damage:** subject to a €500 excess (to prevent low-value abuse claims)
- **Pain and suffering (Schmerzensgeld), medical treatment, loss of earnings:** same as standard liability

→ Apply online at verkehrsopferhilfe.de – maximum limitation period: 3 years from the date of knowledge.

## Criminal Consequences for the At-Fault Party

§ 142 StGB (unauthorised departure from the scene of an accident): up to 3 years' imprisonment or a fine. If the perpetrator is identified, criminal proceedings run in parallel with civil claims.

## What the Insurance Typically Claims

> *"At-fault party unknown – no entitlement."*

Incorrect – Verkehrsopferhilfe steps in. You must proactively submit the application yourself.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who pays if the at-fault driver has fled?**
Where the at-fault party cannot be identified: Verkehrsopferhilfe e.V. under § 12 PflVG.

**What do I need to keep in mind for the VOH application?**
File a police report within 7 days, make demonstrable efforts to identify the perpetrator, and submit the application on time.

**Is there an excess?**
For property damage: yes, €500. For personal injury: no.

**Can I still claim Schmerzensgeld (pain and suffering) after a hit-and-run?**
Yes, in full – VOH coverage mirrors standard liability.
