# Manufacturer Product Liability for Vehicle Defects

> **In brief:** If a **vehicle or component defect** contributed to an accident (e.g. brake failure, airbag misfire, steering defect), the **manufacturer** may be liable in addition to the vehicle keeper — under the **Produkthaftungsgesetz (ProdHaftG)** (German Product Liability Act) and § 823 BGB. Injured parties can have two simultaneous claim defendants: the keeper's liability insurer AND the manufacturer. In practice, manufacturer liability is harder to prove, but worthwhile in cases involving serious harm.

## Also Known As

ProdHaftG · Hersteller-Haftung · Fahrzeug-Mangel-Haftung · design/manufacturing/instruction defect

## Requirements

ProdHaftG requires:
1. **Product defect** within the meaning of § 3 ProdHaftG
2. **Damage** caused by the defect
3. **Placing on the market** of the product by the manufacturer

**Types of defect:**
- **Design defect (Konstruktions-Fehler)** – error already present in the design
- **Manufacturing defect (Fabrikations-Fehler)** – individual defective unit
- **Instruction defect (Instruktions-Fehler)** – missing or inadequate warnings

## Parallel Bases for Claims

| Basis for Claim | Against | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| § 7 StVG | Vehicle keeper | No fault required |
| § 18 StVG | Driver | Fault presumed |
| § 823 BGB | Manufacturer | Fault (presumed under Produzentenhaftung) |
| ProdHaftG | Manufacturer | Product defect |

→ The injured party can pursue claims **cumulatively**; insurer and manufacturer settle between themselves.

## What Must Be Proved

Under classic Produzentenhaftung (§ 823 BGB), the burden of proof regarding the manufacturer's fault is **reduced** for the injured party: where a product is demonstrably defective, the manufacturer's fault is **presumed**. Under ProdHaftG, liability without fault is even possible — the only requirements are a product defect and causation.

## Typical Cases

- **Brake failure** with no maintenance gap
- **Airbag misfire**
- **Steering defect** in a new vehicle
- **Tyre blowout** caused by a Fabrikations-Fehler
- **Unintended acceleration** (e.g. Toyota cases in past years)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the manufacturer bear liability for a vehicle defect?**
Yes — under the Produkthaftungsgesetz and § 823 BGB, in parallel with keeper liability under the StVG.

**What do I need to prove?**
Product defect and causation of the damage. The manufacturer's fault is presumed.

**Who is the manufacturer for an imported vehicle?**
The manufacturer within the meaning of § 4 ProdHaftG — for imported vehicles, often the German distribution company, or alternatively the foreign manufacturer.

**Is a claim against the manufacturer worth pursuing?**
For substantial losses, yes — a specialist lawyer is essential; this is highly complex legal territory.
