# Haushaltsführungsschaden – When an Accident Disrupts Your Household

> **In brief:** Haushaltsführungsschaden (household management loss) is compensation for the value of domestic work you **can no longer perform yourself due to accident-related injury** — regardless of whether you actually hire external help or family members step in. Calculated using the **Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle** with a notional net hourly rate (typically €10–14) × hours lost × duration of incapacity. An independent claim alongside loss of earnings and pain and suffering damages.

## Also known as

Haushaltsführungs-Schaden · Haushaltsführungspauschale · Schulz-Borck-Schaden · MdH (Minderung der Haushaltsführung)

---

## Explained in one sentence

Haushaltsführungsschaden is the standardised value of household work you can no longer perform after an accident — calculated using the Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle.

## Explained in three sentences

If you are temporarily or permanently restricted in running your own household following a road traffic accident, you are entitled to an **independent monetary claim** — Haushaltsführungsschaden under § 843 BGB (German Civil Code, for injured parties) or § 844 Abs. 2 BGB (for surviving dependants). The reference framework is the **Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle**, which assigns a typical weekly hour requirement based on household size and number of occupants; this is multiplied by a notional hourly rate (typically €10–14 net, benchmarked against the minimum wage and local market rates) and the accident-related percentage reduction in household capacity (MdH). The claim exists **regardless of whether you actually hire a household assistant** — notional calculation is permissible under established BGH case law.

## The five key figures

- **§ 843 BGB** — legal basis (injured party)
- **§ 844 Abs. 2 BGB** — surviving dependants variant
- **Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle** — standard judicial reference
- **€10–14/hr** — typical notional net hourly rate
- **MdH-%** — degree of reduction in household capacity (Minderung der Haushaltsführung)

---

## Who is entitled

- **All persons managing a household** — regardless of employment status
- **Employed persons**: in addition to loss of earnings
- **Homemakers**: instead of loss of earnings (no employment income lost, but full Haushaltsführungsschaden applies)
- **Single-person households**: pro-rata — your own household counts
- **Partners / families**: in a shared household, with apportionment

→ The claim exists even without actually hiring external help (notional calculation under BGH case law).

---

## How the Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle works

The table assigns **weekly hours** to typical household types:

| Household occupants | Typical hrs/week | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 person | 25–30 | Small household |
| 2 persons | 35–45 | Typical couples |
| 3 persons (e.g. with 1 child) | 45–55 | Standard family |
| 4+ persons | 55–70 | Multi-child families |
| With young children / care-dependent person | +15–25 | Increased workload |

Exact values depend on the specific household (floor area, children, care needs, garden, lifestyle).

---

## Calculation formula

```
Haushaltsführungsschaden = weekly hrs × hourly rate × weeks
                         × MdH-% (reduction in household capacity)
```

### Example: Moderate whiplash injury (HWS-Distorsion), 3-person household

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Weekly hours (3-person household) | 50 hrs/week |
| Injured party's share of household management | 60% = 30 hrs/week |
| MdH (reduction) | 50% (partially restricted) |
| Actual hours lost per week | 15 hrs |
| Hourly rate | €12/hr net |
| Period of incapacity | 8 weeks |
| **Haushaltsführungsschaden** | **15 × 12 × 8 = €1,440** |

---

## MdH-% — reduction levels

The insurer (or court / expert appraiser) assesses how severely your household capacity is restricted:

| MdH | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | Completely unable | Bedridden, severe injury, hospitalisation |
| 75% | Severely restricted | Major mobility impairment, arm in plaster cast |
| 50% | Moderately restricted | Moderate HWS-Distorsion (whiplash), limited mobility |
| 25% | Mildly restricted | Recovery under way, some tasks possible |
| 0% | Fully restored | Recovery complete |

In practice: MdH levels are typically graduated across the recovery phase, each with its own time period.

---

## Hourly rate — notional or actual

### Notional calculation (standard)
- €10–14/hr net, benchmarked against minimum wage and local market rates
- No actual hiring of a household assistant required
- BGH case law: notional calculation is permissible

### Actual calculation
- Documented invoices from a hired household assistant
- Higher hourly rates possible (€15–18/hr for professional services)
- Retain all receipts

---

## Evidence requirements

| Item | Evidence |
|---|---|
| MdH-% | Medical certificate; expert appraiser (Sachverständiger) if disputed |
| Duration of incapacity | Sick note (AU-Bescheinigung) |
| Household size | Personal statement; registration certificate if required |
| Injured party's share of household management | Sworn declaration (eidesstattliche Versicherung) |
| Actual household assistant (optional) | Invoices, contracts |

§ 287 ZPO (German Code of Civil Procedure) permits a **reduced standard of proof** — lump-sum estimates are permissible.

## What to do now — step by step

### 💡 The most important rule
Haushaltsführungsschaden is one of the **most frequently overlooked** claim positions. You must actively assert it — insurers settle only what is explicitly claimed.

### 🛠 Four steps

**1. Request a medical MdH assessment**
Often already implicit in the sick note — if disputed, request a separate medical certificate stating "Minderung der Haushaltsführung um X %" (reduction in household capacity by X%).

**2. Document your household situation**
Number of occupants, floor area, children, care needs if applicable. Your share of household management within the family home.

**3. Choose your hourly rate**
If no external help was hired: notional rate of €10–14/hr. If actual help was engaged: documented receipts.

**4. Assert the claim as a separate item in your claim letter**
Clearly identify the position: "Haushaltsführungsschaden nach Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle, [amount] €."

---

## What the insurer typically argues

> *"Haushaltsführungsschaden insufficiently evidenced — no reimbursement."*

Reduced standard of proof under § 287 ZPO. A sworn declaration (eidesstattliche Versicherung) + sick note (AU-Bescheinigung) + household details are sufficient.

> *"You did not hire any help — so there is no loss."*

Incorrect. Notional calculation is permissible under BGH case law — you are not required to have actually hired assistance.

> *"The hourly rate is too high."*

€10–14/hr net is the established judicial standard. Insurer reductions to €6–8/hr are not sustainable.

## Frequently asked questions

**Am I entitled to Haushaltsführungsschaden even if I am not in employment?**
Yes — particularly so. Homemakers (Hausfrauen / Hausmänner) have no conventional loss of earnings, but they are entitled to the full Haushaltsführungsschaden.

**Do I need to have actually hired someone?**
No. Notional calculation under the Schulz-Borck-Hofmann-Tabelle is permissible under BGH case law — including where family members stepped in or where the work remained undone.

**What is the applicable hourly rate?**
Typically €10–14/hr net for notional calculations. Where a household assistant was actually engaged, the amount actually paid (often €15–18/hr).

**What is the MdH?**
Minderung der Haushaltsführung — the percentage reduction in your household capacity. Assessed by a doctor or, if disputed, by an expert appraiser (Sachverständiger).

**Can I claim Haushaltsführungsschaden in addition to loss of earnings?**
Yes — both claims run in parallel. Loss of earnings covers employment income; Haushaltsführungsschaden covers domestic work.

**What if the impairment is permanent?**
The claim is then treated by analogy with [§ 843 BGB Erwerbsminderungsschaden (loss of earning capacity)](/haftpflicht/erwerbsminderungsschaden) — a lifelong annuity or capitalised lump sum.

## If you are unsure how to proceed

Haushaltsführungsschaden goes unclaimed in over 50% of cases — yet for homemakers or families with several children it can amount to €1,000–3,000 per recovery phase. An independent expert appraiser (Sachverständiger) and specialist lawyer (Fachanwalt) are strongly advisable.
