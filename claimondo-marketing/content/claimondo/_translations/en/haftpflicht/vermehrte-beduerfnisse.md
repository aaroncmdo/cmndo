# Increased Needs under § 843 BGB – Ongoing Additional Costs for Permanent Consequences

> **In brief:** Increased needs (Vermehrte Bedürfnisse) are all ongoing additional costs you incur for life — or over an extended period — as a result of permanent injuries: e.g. medication, therapies, care, personal assistance, disability-appropriate home adaptations, and specialist mobility solutions. The claim under § 843 BGB takes the form of a **periodic allowance (Geldrente)** or a lump-sum capitalisation. This is a highly complex item; specialist legal representation is essential.

## Also known as

Mehrbedarf · § 843 BGB needs · ongoing cost of living increases · permanent care and support requirements

---

## Explained in one sentence

Vermehrte Bedürfnisse are ongoing, lifelong or multi-year additional costs resulting from permanent accident-related injuries — recoverable as a periodic allowance or a lump sum.

## Explained in three sentences

If you have been permanently impaired following a road traffic accident, § 843 BGB entitles you to a **periodic allowance (Geldrente)** covering all additional costs permanently associated with your injury: from long-term medication and personal assistance through to disability-appropriate adaptations of your home and vehicle. The allowance is either **capitalised** into a one-off payment or paid on an ongoing basis; the amount is derived from the **specific individual cost items**, each annualised and then multiplied by the expected duration of the need. The calculation is highly complex — involving actuarial tables, life expectancy, and inflation factors — and in cases of polytrauma, severe disability, or spinal cord injury, total claims quickly reach **six-figure sums**.

## The five key figures

- **§ 843 BGB** as the legal basis
- **Lifelong** or duration-specific entitlement
- **Capitalisation possible** (lump sum instead of periodic allowance)
- **Highly complex** — specialist lawyer essential
- **Six figures** — typical total claims for serious injuries

---

## What counts as "increased needs"

### Medical
- Long-term medication
- Replacement of aids (prosthesis, orthosis, hearing aid)
- Lifelong regular therapies
- Adjustments to account for recovery or deterioration

### Personal
- Personal assistance (for severe disability)
- Care support (see [Pflege-Mehrbedarf](/haftpflicht/pflege-mehrbedarf))
- Additional domestic help

### Structural adaptations
- Disability-appropriate home modifications (doors, bathroom, stair lift)
- Disability-appropriate vehicle
- Equipment and aids infrastructure

### Mobility
- Specialist means of transport
- Disability taxi
- Higher travel costs for alternative mobility solutions

---

## How the assessment works

Three steps:

1. **Identification** of all individual cost items (medication, therapies, assistance, adaptations)
2. **Annualisation** of each item
3. **Capitalisation** over the expected duration of need (life expectancy or specific prognosis)

→ Actuarial calculation using mortality tables. Lawyer and expert witnesses essential.

---

## Periodic allowance vs. capitalisation

| Form | Advantage | Disadvantage |
|---|---|---|
| **Ongoing periodic allowance (Geldrente)** | Adjustable to real-life changes | Risk of insurer insolvency |
| **Capitalisation (lump sum)** | Certainty; injured party manages the funds | Inflation risk; harder to vary |

Capitalisation is the most common outcome in practice — the settlement then contains a one-off payment covering the expected duration of need.

## What to do now — step by step

### 💡 The most important rule
Vermehrte Bedürfnisse are **not a DIY matter**. This is a complex area of law; a specialist road traffic or social law lawyer is essential.

### 🛠 Three steps

**1. Comprehensive documentation of consequences**
Medical findings, care-needs certificates, expert reports on your living situation.

**2. Engage a specialist lawyer**
Road traffic law and social law. The opposing insurer covers the legal fees.

**3. Reserve clause in any settlement**
If a settlement is being pursued, insist on a reserve clause for deterioration in your condition — this must be included in the agreement (see [Pauschal-Abgeltung](/decoder/pauschal-abgeltung)).

## Frequently asked questions

**What are Vermehrte Bedürfnisse?**
All ongoing additional costs resulting from permanent accident-related injuries — medication, therapy, care, personal assistance, and adaptation measures.

**How is the amount calculated?**
Each item is annualised and multiplied by the expected duration of need (life expectancy). Actuarial capitalisation applies.

**Will I receive a lump sum or ongoing payments?**
Both are possible. In practice, capitalisation as a lump sum is the most common outcome.

**Who pays for disability-appropriate home adaptations?**
Where a severe disability results from the accident, the opposing insurer bears the cost as part of the Vermehrte Bedürfnisse claim.

**What if my needs worsen over time?**
With a periodic allowance: adjustment is possible. With capitalisation: only if a late-consequences reserve clause was included in the settlement.
