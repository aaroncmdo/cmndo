# Trailer / Caravan / Semi-Trailer – Liability in an Accident

> **In brief:** Trailers with their own registration (caravans, horse trailers, boat trailers, semi-trailers) require **their own motor vehicle liability insurance** (§ 1 PflVG). In an accident: both the **towing vehicle keeper** and the **trailer keeper** are liable under § 7 StVG – the apportionment of liability is determined by the specific circumstances of causation. For trailers not subject to registration requirements (small trailers up to 750 kg without brakes), the towing vehicle's motor liability insurance typically provides coverage.

## Also Known As

§ 19a StVG · Anhängerhaftung · Wohnwagen-Versicherung · Auflieger-Konstellation

## Insurance Requirement

| Trailer Type | Own Liability Policy? |
|---|---|
| Semi-trailer / heavy trailer | Yes, own policy required |
| Caravan / touring caravan | Yes, own policy required |
| Boat/horse trailer > 750 kg | Yes, own policy required |
| Small trailer ≤ 750 kg without brakes | Covered under towing vehicle |

## Apportionment in an Accident

| Scenario | Liability Distribution |
|---|---|
| Towing vehicle at fault, trailer swings | Primary liability: towing vehicle keeper |
| Trailer breaks away despite correct towing vehicle | Trailer keeper bears a share |
| Loose load falling from trailer | Both jointly liable (§ 421 BGB) |
| Trailer rear-ends without braking | Towing vehicle keeper |

→ **§ 19a StVG**: trailer and towing vehicle are treated as a **single unit** – the two-insurer arrangement is settled internally between the insurers.

## What to Bear in Mind After a Trailer Accident

1. Notify **both the towing vehicle insurer and the trailer insurer**
2. Provide the **addresses of both insurers** to the injured party
3. **§ 19a StVG logic**: joint liability of both insurers
4. **Internal indemnification** is a matter between the insurers – not your concern

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do trailers require their own liability insurance?**
Yes, if they have their own registration. Small trailers up to 750 kg without brakes are covered under the towing vehicle's policy.

**Who is liable in an accident involving a caravan?**
Both – the towing vehicle keeper and the caravan keeper (often the same person). For any loss, the relevant insurer pays.

**What if the trailer belongs to someone else (hired or rented trailer)?**
The keeper (hire company or rental firm) is liable as the trailer keeper; the hirer may additionally be liable as the driver.
