# Third Parties – Pedestrians, Cyclists, Children in Road Accidents

> **In brief:** Not only vehicle occupants are injured parties – **pedestrians, cyclists, children** and **other road users** hold full claims in road accidents involving a motor vehicle. For pedestrians and cyclists, the lower Betriebsgefahr (operational hazard) of non-motorised road users works in their favour – contributory negligence findings are rarer and lower than between motor vehicles. **Children under 10** are not tortiously liable under § 828 Abs. 2 BGB (German Civil Code) in road traffic – no contributory negligence is possible.

## Also Known As

Nicht-motorisierte Verkehrsteilnehmer · Schutzbedürftige Verkehrsteilnehmer · Vulnerable Road Users

## Scenarios and Liability

### Pedestrians
- Standard: 100 : 0 against the vehicle owner (Betriebsgefahr)
- Pedestrian right of way at zebra crossings (§ 26 StVO) – full protection
- Pedestrian fault (red light, stepping suddenly into the road) – contributory share 20–50 %
- **Children under 10**: 0 % contributory negligence possible

### Cyclists
- Standard: 80 : 20 to 100 : 0 against the motor vehicle
- Own Betriebsgefahr of a bicycle: low to zero
- Special cases: [door opening](/haftpflicht/tueroeffnen) (100 : 0), [overtaking without safe clearance](/haftpflicht/ueberholen)
- Helmet absence: BGH case law – NO, no contributory negligence solely due to missing helmet

### E-Scooters / Pedelecs
- Treated like bicycles (pedelec up to 25 km/h)
- E-scooter: own liability insurance obligation may apply
- Otherwise treated as standard road users

## Children in Road Traffic – § 828 Abs. 2 BGB

Key provision:

> *"A person who has reached the age of seven but not yet the age of ten is not liable for damage caused to another person in an accident involving a motor vehicle … unless the injury was caused intentionally."*

→ **Children under 10**: **No contributory negligence** in a road accident involving a motor vehicle.
→ **Ages 10–18**: Contributory negligence depending on capacity for insight (§ 828 Abs. 3 BGB).
→ **Parental duty of supervision** may apply separately (§ 832 BGB) – but is rarely raised as an argument by the at-fault party in practice.

## Claims of Third Parties

Identical to those of vehicle occupants (see [Primary injured parties](/haftpflicht/geschaedigte-primaer)):
- [Schmerzensgeld (pain and suffering) § 253 BGB](/haftpflicht/schmerzensgeld-bgb253)
- [Medical treatment costs](/haftpflicht/heilbehandlungskosten)
- [Loss of earnings](/haftpflicht/verdienstausfall)
- Property damage (bicycle, clothing, smartphone)
- [Vermehrte Bedürfnisse (increased needs)](/haftpflicht/vermehrte-beduerfnisse) in cases of serious injury

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is liable in an accident involving a pedestrian?**
Typically the vehicle owner – Betriebsgefahr. Contributory share for pedestrian fault: 20–50 %.

**What happens when a child is struck by a vehicle?**
For children under 10: no contributory negligence – full claim.

**Does riding without a helmet count as contributory negligence for cyclists?**
No, under the BGH line of case law. There is no statutory helmet requirement for cyclists – riding without a helmet does not constitute contributory negligence.

**Who pays Schmerzensgeld for an injured cyclist?**
The liability insurance (Haftpflichtversicherung) of the vehicle owner – typically full reimbursement given the prima facie evidence (Anscheinsbeweis) constellation.
