# Verkehrsopferhilfe e.V. – When It Steps In

> **In brief:** Verkehrsopferhilfe (VOH) e.V. is the **guarantee fund of the German Insurance Association** — it steps in when an injured party with a legitimate claim has no other liable party to turn to. Four main scenarios: unknown at-fault party (hit-and-run), uninsured at-fault party, insurer insolvency, and intentionally unlawful use of a vehicle (theft / unauthorised driving).

## Also Known As

VOH · § 12 PflVG · Garantiefonds Kfz

## Four Trigger Scenarios

| Scenario | VOH Coverage |
|---|---|
| **Hit-and-run** (unknown at-fault party) | Full, minus €500 excess for property damage |
| **Uninsured at-fault party** | Full, minus excess for property damage |
| **Insurer insolvency** | Full, no excess |
| **Theft / unauthorised driving** | Full, minus excess for property damage |

## Excess (Deductible)

- **Property damage:** €500 (to prevent frivolous minor claims)
- **Personal injury:** no excess — fully covered

## Application Requirements

1. **Police report** including damage documentation
2. **Active efforts** to identify the at-fault party (witness searches, licence plate enquiries)
3. **Claim submitted within 3 years** (standard limitation period)
4. **Evidence** for all damage items
5. **Existing comprehensive insurance (Vollkasko)** must be used first (for property damage)

→ Apply directly at [verkehrsopferhilfe.de](https://www.verkehrsopferhilfe.de) — online form available.

## Process

1. Submit application with police report
2. VOH reviews eligibility (typically 4–8 weeks)
3. If approved: settlement proceeds as with standard third-party liability
4. If the at-fault party is later identified: VOH pursues recourse against them

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When does Verkehrsopferhilfe pay?**
When the at-fault party is unknown or uninsured, when the insurer is insolvent, or in theft / unauthorised-driving scenarios.

**How high is the excess?**
€500 for property damage; none for personal injury.

**How quickly is payment made?**
Typically 8–16 weeks after a complete application is submitted.
