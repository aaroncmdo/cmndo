# Additional Care Needs After a Road Accident

> **In brief:** If an injured party becomes dependent on care as a result of an accident, all **additional care costs** are fully reimbursable under § 843 BGB (German Civil Code on compensation for increased needs) — minus any benefits paid by the statutory long-term care insurance, which subrogates under § 116 SGB X (German Social Code Book X). Both **professional care services** and **notional compensation for caring family members** (€10–14/hour for home care) are reimbursable. In cases of permanent care dependency, capitalised lump-sum figures in the six-figure range are realistic.

## Also Known As

Care costs · Care-related damage · Family carer reimbursement · Additional home care needs

---

## In One Sentence

Additional care needs (Pflege-Mehrbedarf) is the reimbursement of all care costs arising from accident-induced care dependency — whether provided by professionals or by family members.

## In Three Sentences

If an accident leaves you dependent on care, you are entitled to reimbursement of your **total additional care needs** under § 843 BGB — from daily personal care through medical treatment care to 24-hour assistance in cases of severe dependency. The statutory long-term care insurance (Pflegekasse) pays according to the applicable care grade (Pflegegrad) and then subrogate against the insurer under § 116 SGB X; all costs beyond those amounts — private care, high-quality assistance, specialist severe-care services — are independently reimbursable. **Family-member care** is also compensable: notional compensation of €10–14/hour for home-based personal care under established BGH (Federal Court of Justice) case law, even where family members receive no actual payment.

## The Five Key Figures

- **§ 843 BGB** — legal basis for additional care needs claims
- **§ 116 SGB X** — subrogation by the long-term care insurer
- **€10–14/hour** — notional compensation for family carers
- **Care grades 1–5 (Pflegegrad 1–5)** — reference scale for assessment
- **Six-figure sums** — typical capitalised amounts in severe-care cases

---

## Who Is Entitled to Additional Care Needs Compensation

- Accident victims with permanent care dependency (polytrauma, severe traumatic brain injury, spinal cord injury, serious chronic sequelae)
- Temporary care needs also covered (e.g. six months following complex surgery)

---

## What Counts as Additional Care Needs

| Area | Example |
|---|---|
| **Personal care (Grundpflege)** | Washing, dressing, mobility assistance, nutrition |
| **Medical treatment care (Behandlungspflege)** | Medication administration, wound care, injections |
| **Household management** | Shopping, cooking, cleaning (see also [Loss of Household Services](/haftpflicht/haushaltsfuehrungsschaden)) |
| **Mobility** | Accompaniment to appointments, transport services |
| **24-hour assistance** | For severe care dependency — often the Polish live-in care model or residential placement |

## Family Carer Compensation — No Invoice Required

An important line of BGH case law: even where family members provide care **free of charge**, the care need is assessed on a **notional** basis and must be reimbursed by the insurer.

### Notional Hourly Rates (Guidance)
- €10–14/hour for home-based personal care by family members
- €15–20/hour for professional care by a care service
- €22–30/hour for specialist severe-care services

→ The notional compensation **does not** have to be actually paid to the family members — the claim exists against the insurer regardless.

---

## Long-Term Care Insurance Share — Subrogation

The statutory long-term care insurer (Pflegekasse) pays first according to care grade:

| Care Grade (Pflegegrad) | Care Allowance (home care) | Care-in-Kind Benefit (care service) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | – | €125/month (relief amount) |
| 2 | €332/month | €760/month |
| 3 | €573/month | €1,432/month |
| 4 | €765/month | €1,778/month |
| 5 | €947/month | €2,200/month |

(2024 figures — subject to regular adjustment.)

The Pflegekasse recovers these amounts from the insurer via § 116 SGB X subrogation — you claim only the **additional costs** beyond that threshold.

## What to Do Now — Concrete Steps

### 💡 The Most Important Rule
For permanent care needs, engaging a specialist lawyer is essential — the calculation is complex, and insurers' initial offers typically run 30–50 % below the amount you are legally entitled to.

### 🛠 Four Steps

**1. Apply for a care grade (Pflegegrad)** (with your Pflegekasse)
Even if the need is temporary — the care grade provides essential documentation.

**2. Document care hours**
Keep a daily care diary: what was done, by whom, for how many hours.

**3. Specialist lawyer**
Road traffic law combined with social law. Engage a calculation expert for litigation.

**4. Reserve clause for late sequelae in any settlement**
Mandatory where there is a risk of future deterioration.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who pays for care after a road accident?**
The Pflegekasse pays according to care grade and subrogate against the insurer. All additional costs beyond that are reimbursable directly by the insurer — including notional family carer compensation.

**Do I receive money for care provided by family members?**
Yes. Notional compensation of €10–14/hour for home-based personal care under established BGH case law — even where no actual payment is made to family members.

**What if I do not yet have a care grade?**
Apply for a Pflegegrad — even for temporary care dependency, the formal assessment decision is worthwhile.

**How much does 24-hour care cost?**
Depending on the model, €2,500–6,000/month. Fully reimbursable where there is a medical indication.

**Is litigation worthwhile in care-related disputes?**
For permanent care dependency, yes — amounts quickly reach six figures.
