# Rental Car Reimbursement After a Traffic Accident

> **Quick summary:** If your vehicle is being repaired or you are waiting for a replacement after an accident that was not your fault, you are entitled to a rental car — the costs are covered by the opposing party's liability insurer (§ 249 BGB). The **"standard local market rate"** is the benchmark, calculated by courts as an arithmetic average of the Schwacke-Liste and the Fraunhofer-Mietpreisspiegel. Rental car class: at most **one category below** your own vehicle. Duration corresponds to the **actual repair or replacement period**.

## Also known as

Mietwagen-Anspruch · Ersatzfahrzeug · rental car reimbursement · UET (Unfall-Ersatz-Tarif)

---

## Explained in one sentence

Rental car costs are reimbursable when your vehicle is out of service due to an accident — daily rate based on the standard local market rate, class at most one category below your own vehicle, duration matching the actual repair period.

## Explained in three sentences

If you are not at fault and your vehicle is unavailable, you are entitled to a rental car as a replacement — provided the repair or replacement period applies, no second vehicle is available, and the rental class is appropriate (BGH line). The reimbursable daily rate is based on the **"standard local market rate"**, calculated in court practice as an arithmetic average of the **Schwacke-Liste** (higher daily rates) and the **Fraunhofer-Mietpreisspiegel** (lower daily rates). Maximum rental class: your own vehicle's category — one class lower is an acceptable duty to mitigate damages, multiple classes lower is not.

## The five key figures

- **§ 249 BGB** as the legal basis for the claim
- **14 days** standard replacement period in total-loss cases
- **Arithmetic average** of Schwacke + Fraunhofer as the BGH reference
- **1 class** reduction acceptable (duty to mitigate)
- **€0 cost** to you in a no-fault accident

---

## Requirements for a rental car claim

Four conditions must be met:

1. **Your own vehicle is out of service** — under repair or awaiting replacement
2. **No second vehicle available** (see below)
3. **Rental actually taken** (unlike the alternative [Nutzungsausfall](/haftpflicht/nutzungsausfall) claim)
4. **Rental class appropriate** (at most your own vehicle's class)

If all conditions are met, the rental invoice is fully reimbursable — and where a cheaper rate was not available at the time of rental, even the higher UET (Unfall-Ersatz-Tarif) is reimbursable.

---

## Schwacke vs. Fraunhofer — the BGH line

The two leading reference tables for assessing the "standard local market rate":

| Source | Data basis | Tendency |
|---|---|---|
| **Schwacke-Liste** (Eurotax) | Survey of rental companies (walk-in rates) | Higher daily rates |
| **Fraunhofer-Mietpreisspiegel** | Online booking platforms with advance reservation | Lower daily rates |
| **Arithmetic average of both** | Combined mean | BGH line in most OLG districts |

The BGH accepts **both tables as valid estimation bases** — courts predominantly use the arithmetic average. Insurers selectively cite whichever table is more favourable to them — see [Decoder "Mietwagen-Tagessatz zu hoch"](/decoder/mietwagen-zu-hoch).

---

## Rental car class — what is reasonable

The BGH line on "appropriate class":

- **Own mid-range vehicle** → mid-range rental reimbursable (Class C)
- **One class below** is acceptable (Class B instead of Class C) — duty to mitigate damages
- **Multiple classes below** is not acceptable
- **Premium vehicle (Class E+)** → possibly only mid-range reimbursable (upper limit of the acceptable reduction)

→ In practice: if you drive a mid-range car, a mid-range rental is fully reimbursable. Insurer pressure to accept a compact or city car is not permissible.

---

## Rental duration — what is reimbursed

The **actual** period of unavailability is the benchmark:

- **For repairs:** workshop order and repair report
- **For total loss:** approx. 14 days replacement period (BGH standard)
- **For specialist vehicles or extended delivery times:** longer period can be documented
- **For spare-parts waiting time:** extends the repair duration

Insurer reductions citing "a flat rate of only 7 days" or "the repair should have been completed faster" are frequently unfounded when the actual duration is documented by workshop records.

---

## Rental car or Nutzungsausfall — your choice

You can either rent a replacement vehicle or claim the flat-rate [Nutzungsausfall](/haftpflicht/nutzungsausfall) — not both simultaneously. A practical comparison:

| | Rental car | Nutzungsausfall |
|---|---|---|
| Requirement | Rental actually taken | Presumption of personal use |
| Amount | Actual daily rate | Sanden-Danner flat rate |
| Mobility | Maintained | Not maintained |
| Effort | Rental process and administration | None |
| With second vehicle | Reduced | Reduced |

→ Case-by-case decision: if you need mobility, a rental car is the better option. If you can manage through a second vehicle, public transport, or a bicycle, Nutzungsausfall is often more economical (flat rate with no actual costs incurred).

## What to do now — step by step

### 💡 The most important rule
Document the rental with proof of rate availability. If a cheaper rate was not available at the time of rental, the higher rate actually paid is reimbursable.

### 🛠 Four steps

**1. Document the rental**
Rental agreement, daily rate, class, duration.

**2. Document rate availability**
Which providers were contacted? Which rates were available? Screenshots of online comparisons.

**3. Document the repair duration**
Workshop order, repair report, vehicle return date.

**4. Respond to insurer reductions using the Decoder**
For reductions above €100, see [Decoder "Mietwagen-Tagessatz zu hoch"](/decoder/mietwagen-zu-hoch).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who pays for the rental car after an accident?**
In a no-fault accident, the opposing party's liability insurer covers the cost as a damage item under § 249 BGB. You bear no financial risk.

**How long can the rental period be?**
For the actual repair duration (documented by workshop records), or in total-loss cases typically 14 days for the replacement period.

**Which rental car class can I choose?**
At most the class of your own vehicle; one class below is an acceptable duty to mitigate damages.

**What is the UET (Unfall-Ersatz-Tarif)?**
A higher rental rate specifically for accident victims (the rental company carries the pre-financing risk). Reimbursable only if a cheaper rate was **not available** at the time of rental.

**What if I have a second vehicle?**
If the second vehicle is genuinely available, the rental car claim may be reduced or excluded — depending on the specific household situation.

**Can I receive compensation without renting a car?**
Yes — alternatively, [Nutzungsausfall](/haftpflicht/nutzungsausfall) (Sanden-Danner flat rate). You choose one or the other.
