# HUK24: Claim Settlement, Direct Brand, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** HUK24 is the online direct brand of the HUK-Coburg Group and processes claims through the same operation in Coburg as HUK-Coburg Allgemeine. In documented cases, the same reduction patterns apply — plus the recurring recommendation to obtain only a cost estimate (Kostenvoranschlag) rather than an independent expert report (Gutachten). The BaFin complaint ratio for 2024 stands at 1.89 per 100,000 contracts. This hub shows you how to secure your claims.

## How HUK24 Settles Third-Party Claims

HUK24 is not an independent claims handler — it is the **direct brand of the HUK-Coburg Group**, sharing the same operation in Coburg. Documented cases therefore follow the parent company's patterns: reductions to the expert's fee (Sachverständigenhonorar) and ancillary workshop costs, and rental car (Mietwagen) rates calculated using an internal table rather than the Schwacke index.

A characteristic feature of HUK24 is an **initial letter sent shortly after the accident** that steers claimants toward a cost estimate (Kostenvoranschlag) rather than an independent expert report (Gutachten) — documented on unfall-lexikon.de. This is problematic for injured parties (Geschädigte): a cost estimate typically does not cover diminished value (Wertminderung), loss of use (Nutzungsausfall), vehicle transport charges (Verbringung), or UPE surcharges — the difference compared to a full expert report is, in practice, frequently in the four-figure range.

Several district courts (Amtsgerichte) have rejected HUK24 reductions: the AG Speyer (31 C 272/15, 02.10.2015) on rental car costs (Schwacke) and expert fees, the AG Mayen (2d C 403/16, 16.12.2016) on transport charges based on actual invoicing, and the AG Grimma (4 C 154/11, 09.07.2013) on outstanding rental car and expert costs.

The **escalation pattern** mirrors the group: full payment out of court is frequently withheld; once a claim is filed against the policyholder, payment is often made at short notice. The BaFin complaint ratio for 2024 stands at 1.89 per 100,000 contracts (109 complaints), slightly below the industry average of 2.2 — HUK24 shares the claims operation of the HUK-Coburg Group, which was named a problem insurer by 68% of traffic law attorneys in the 2017 Forsa survey (AG Verkehrsrecht im DAV).

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against HUK24

1. **Expert report, not a cost estimate.** Do not let yourself be steered toward a Kostenvoranschlag. As an injured party who bears no fault, you are entitled to your own independent expert (§ 249 BGB) — the costs are borne by the opposing liability insurer.
2. **Claim every item.** Insist on Wertminderung, Nutzungsausfall, Verbringung, and UPE surcharges where the expert report supports them.
3. **Set a deadline.** A specific payment deadline triggers default (Verzug) under § 286 BGB once it expires, including statutory default interest.
4. **Enforce through our partner law firm.** Given the group's pattern of "payment only after legal action," legal pressure significantly increases the success rate — where liability is clear, HUK24 bears the legal costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is HUK24 the same as HUK-Coburg?**
HUK24 is the online direct brand of the HUK-Coburg Group and uses the same claims operation in Coburg. In third-party claim settlement, the same patterns apply as with HUK-Coburg Allgemeine.

**Should I obtain the cost estimate HUK24 recommends?**
Better not: a Kostenvoranschlag typically does not cover Wertminderung, Nutzungsausfall, or other claim items. You are entitled to a full, independent Gutachten — the costs are borne by the opposing liability insurer (§ 249 BGB).

**Does HUK24 reduce claims differently from HUK-Coburg?**
No — because of the shared operation, the patterns are virtually identical. Multiple district courts (AG Speyer, AG Mayen, AG Grimma) have rejected HUK24 reductions.

**What is HUK24's BaFin complaint ratio?**
For 2024, it stands at 1.89 per 100,000 contracts (109 complaints), just below the industry average of 2.2 — with a shared claims operation alongside the HUK-Coburg Group.
