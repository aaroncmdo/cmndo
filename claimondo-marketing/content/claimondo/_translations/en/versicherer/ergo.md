# ERGO: Claim Settlement, Carexpert, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** ERGO Versicherung AG (Munich Re Group) recorded the highest BaFin complaint ratio among major motor insurers in 2024 — 4.7 complaints per 100,000 policies (125 complaints total, against an industry average of 2.2). ERGO assesses third-party claims exclusively through its group-affiliated service provider Carexpert; documented practices include inflated residual value assessments via Carexpert re-inspections. This hub explains how to protect your claims.

## How ERGO Settles Third-Party Claims

The defining figure is the **BaFin ratio of 4.7** complaints per 100,000 policies (2024) — the highest recorded among major motor insurers (transparent-beraten.de, based on BaFin data). With approximately 2.7 million policies, that equates to 125 complaints, well above the industry average of 2.2.

In practice, ERGO relies exclusively on **Carexpert** (Munich Re Group, operating exclusively for ERGO and DKV). A particularly consequential documented practice is the **residual value inflation via Carexpert re-inspection**. The law firm Schleyer describes a case (December 2016) in which the residual value (Restwert) was raised from €0 to €34,620, after which a five-figure sum was claimed back from the policyholder. An artificially inflated residual value substantially reduces the total-loss payout — however, the legally binding benchmark is the **regional market residual value**, not a supra-regional salvage exchange bid.

Further documented: blanket **diminished value (Wertminderung) reductions to Carexpert standard rates** and revised repair duration estimates. The Amtsgericht Heinsberg (18 C 403/13, 04.02.2014) rejected an ERGO expert fee reduction; case law shows that ERGO routinely concedes once legal proceedings are initiated (AG Mitte Berlin: back-payment of €619.43 plus legal costs).

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against ERGO

1. **Check the residual value.** Do not accept a supra-regional salvage exchange bid. The legally binding figure is the regional market residual value stated in your expert report (Gutachten) — a re-inspection does not change this.
2. **Treat your own expert report as the benchmark.** Carexpert assessments represent the insurer's position, not a neutral expert report (§ 249 BGB).
3. **Set a payment deadline.** Specify a firm payment deadline; once it expires, ERGO is in default under § 286 BGB.
4. **Escalate to a lawyer early.** The high complaint ratio and the documented pattern of conceding only after legal action is filed make early legal representation advisable — where liability is clear, ERGO bears the legal costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is it true that ERGO has the highest complaint ratio?**
Among major motor insurers, yes: the 2024 BaFin statistics record 4.7 complaints per 100,000 policies for ERGO (125 complaints total) — above the industry average of 2.2.

**What is the residual value inflation via Carexpert?**
In documented cases, a re-inspection is used to establish a higher residual value (Restwert), which reduces the total-loss payout. The legally binding benchmark, however, is the regional market residual value from your expert report — not a supra-regional exchange bid.

**Is ERGO permitted to reduce my diminished value (Wertminderung) claim via Carexpert?**
ERGO may contest it, but your independent expert report remains the binding reference. Multiple courts have rejected ERGO's reductions (e.g., AG Heinsberg 18 C 403/13).
