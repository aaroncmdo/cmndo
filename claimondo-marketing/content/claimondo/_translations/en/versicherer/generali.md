# Generali: Claim Settlement, Workshop Steering, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** Generali Deutschland Versicherung AG relies heavily on its partner workshop network in third-party claim settlement and reviews repair invoices through ClaimsControlling and Carexpert. In documented cases, full settlement has in some instances only followed service of legal proceedings. The official BaFin motor insurance complaint ratio for 2024 is not reported separately. This hub shows you how to protect your claims.

## How Generali Settles Third-Party Claims

The defining feature is **workshop steering through the Generali partner network**. The initial letter frequently uses a "free collection and delivery service" as an inducement to direct the injured party to a partner workshop — where repairs are calculated at reduced rates and positions such as Wertminderung (diminished value) or blending costs are more easily cut. Your **free choice of workshop** remains unaffected by this.

**ClaimsControlling and Carexpert** are deployed for invoice review. When settling on a notional (non-repair) basis, UPE surcharges and transport costs are standard items for reduction. In documented case law (captain-huk.de), Generali frequently settles promptly once legal proceedings are served against the policyholder; the AG Koblenz (161 C 611/13, 29.07.2013) and the AG Aachen (120 C 168/14, 31.07.2014, AachenMünchener/Generali) ordered payment of outstanding expert fees.

On the group structure: **CosmosDirekt** is Generali's direct subsidiary. The frequently confused **DA Direkt belongs to the Zurich Group**, not to Generali — a widespread misconception that matters when assessing whether the two share a claims operation.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against Generali

1. **Exercise your free choice of workshop.** Do not let yourself be steered into a partner workshop. You are entitled to choose your own workshop freely; the opposing liability insurer covers the locally customary repair costs.
2. **Your own expert report as the benchmark.** ClaimsControlling/Carexpert review reports represent the insurer's position — they are not a neutral Gutachten (expert report) within the meaning of § 249 BGB.
3. **Set a deadline.** Specify a concrete payment deadline; once it lapses, the insurer is in default under § 286 BGB.
4. **Payment order / legal action as leverage.** Because Generali frequently settles in full only after service of proceedings, pressure from our partner law firm accelerates payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I have to take my car to a Generali partner workshop?**
No. Free choice of workshop is your right. The "collection and delivery service" is an offer, not an obligation — the opposing liability insurer covers the locally customary repair costs at your chosen workshop.

**Does DA Direkt belong to Generali?**
No — this is a widespread misconception. CosmosDirekt is Generali's direct subsidiary; DA Direkt belongs to the Zurich Group.

**Why does Generali sometimes only pay after legal proceedings are initiated?**
In documented cases, full settlement has frequently occurred only after service of proceedings. Your own Gutachten (expert report) combined with pressure from our partner law firm shortens this path — where liability is clear, Generali bears the costs.
