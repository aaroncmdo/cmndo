# VHV: Claim Settlement, a Policy Decision, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** VHV Allgemeine Versicherung AG is known in the industry for an openly stated position — a "fundamental company policy decision" to calculate expert fees using its own in-house fee calculator, rather than the BVSK-Honorartabelle. In a 2017 DAV-Forsa survey of traffic lawyers, VHV ranked second among problem insurers, cited by 46% of respondents. This page sets out the documented practice soberly and shows which rulings support your claims.

## How VHV Settles Third-Party Claims

VHV's signature approach is the **reduction of expert fees (Sachverständigenhonorar) based on its own in-house fee calculator**. What sets this apart is that VHV openly communicates this position. A documented letter (captain-huk.de, 28.10.2016) states verbatim: "The basis for fee calculation is not the BVSK model, but a developed fee calculator … We maintain the reduction applied to the expert fee invoice on the basis of the factual and legal situation."

Also revealing is a statement concerning the auditing service provider: law firm Voigt (RA Hamann) quotes VHV as stating that the auditing service provider acts under instructions ("weisungsgebunden"), that the audit is based on rules "specified by VHV", and that the service provider has "no independent scope for assessment" ("keinen eigenen Prüfungsspielraum"). This is a strong argument against the evidentiary value of such audit reports — they reflect the insurer's own specifications, not an independent assessment.

**VHV's litigation style** is confrontational and court-ready. The AG Offenbach (36 C 248/15, 04.02.2016) and AG Chemnitz (15 C 928/15, 30.07.2015) both ruled in favour of injured parties (Geschädigte) in cases involving VHV fee reductions; the AG Salzgitter (22 C 57/15, 14.10.2015) found a ControlExpert audit report to be an unsuitable piece of evidence. Once a claim is brought against the policyholder directly, VHV frequently pays without delay.

In the Forsa survey of 1,072 traffic lawyers (AG Verkehrsrecht im DAV, Stern 49/2017), VHV ranked second with **46%**, behind HUK-Coburg (68%). The BaFin complaint ratio for 2024 stands at 1.87 per 100,000 contracts — just below the industry average of 2.2.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against VHV

1. **Reject the in-house fee calculator.** An internal fee calculator is not a binding benchmark. Under BGH case law, the BVSK-Honorarbefragung is a recognised basis for estimation under § 287 ZPO — your expert fee (Gutachterhonorar) is recoverable damage under § 249 BGB (German Civil Code, full restitution).
2. **Challenge the audit report.** Use VHV's own statement: an auditing service provider that is "weisungsgebunden" (acting under instructions) with "no independent scope for assessment" does not produce a neutral expert opinion.
3. **Set a payment deadline.** Issue a concrete payment deadline; once it passes, VHV is in default under § 286 BGB.
4. **Enforce through our partner law firm.** Given VHV's court-ready approach, legal pressure is often the fastest route — where liability is clear, VHV bears the costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is VHV permitted to reduce my fee using its own in-house calculator?**
An internal fee calculator is not a binding benchmark. The BGH recognises the BVSK-Honorarbefragung as a valid basis for estimation; your necessary expert fee (Gutachterhonorar) is recoverable under § 249 BGB.

**What use is VHV's statement about its auditing service provider?**
VHV has acknowledged that the auditing service provider is "weisungsgebunden" (acting under instructions) and has "keinen eigenen Prüfungsspielraum" (no independent scope for assessment) — as cited by law firm Voigt. This is a strong argument against the evidentiary value of such an audit report.

**How does VHV fare in lawyer surveys and with BaFin?**
In the 2017 Forsa survey, VHV ranked second among problem insurers, cited by 46% of respondents. The BaFin complaint ratio for 2024 stands at 1.87 — just below the industry average of 2.2.
