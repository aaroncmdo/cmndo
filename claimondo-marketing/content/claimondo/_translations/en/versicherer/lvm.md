# LVM: Claim Settlement, BGH Citations, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** LVM Sach (Versicherungsverein auf Gegenseitigkeit, Münster) holds the industry-best BaFin complaint ratio of 0.76. Documented cases nonetheless include reduction letters citing BGH rulings that decide the opposite of what is claimed, as well as "passive legal protection" letters directed at expert appraisers. This hub contextualises the documented individual cases without concealing the insurer's overall low complaint density.

## How LVM Settles Third-Party Claims

The most notable pattern in legal practice is **LVM's use of BGH citations in reduction letters that do not support the claimed legal outcome**. Law firm Schleyer documents a case in which LVM reduced expert appraisal ancillary costs by €104.13, citing a BGH ruling dated 22.07.2014 — a ruling that decided the matter differently. Such letters appear legally substantiated but do not withstand scrutiny.

A further pattern is **"passive legal protection" (passiver Rechtsschutz)**: LVM informs legally represented expert appraisers that it is granting its customer passive legal protection and demands that "all correspondence be conducted exclusively with us" (captain-huk.de). This removes the policyholder from the line of fire and channels the dispute directly.

On enforcement, the usual pattern applies: resistant at the pre-litigation stage, then prompt payment once a claim is filed against the policyholder. AG Hagen (11 C 281/13, 22.01.2014) illustrates the economic irrationality of this reduction strategy — a deduction of €14.76 ultimately cost LVM several hundred euros in additional proceedings costs. AG Starnberg ruled in favour of the injured party even in a case involving a young leased vehicle.

**For context (transparency):** With a BaFin ratio of 0.76 per 100,000 contracts, LVM holds the best figure in the industry. The points described above are documented individual cases within an overall below-average complaint density.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against LVM

1. **Have BGH citations verified.** If LVM relies on a ruling, have its actual holding reviewed by a lawyer — in the documented cases, the citation did not support the reduction.
2. **Your own expert report as the benchmark.** The ancillary costs of a necessary Gutachten (expert report) are reimbursable under §249 BGB.
3. **Set a deadline.** Specify a concrete payment deadline; once it lapses, LVM is in default under §286 BGB.
4. **Enforce through a lawyer.** Given the pattern of resistance until litigation, legal pressure is the fastest route — where liability is clear, LVM bears the costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**LVM is citing a BGH ruling — is it accurate?**
In the documented cases, the cited ruling did not support the claimed reduction (law firm Schleyer on the BGH ruling of 22.07.2014). Have the ruling's actual holding reviewed by a lawyer before accepting any reduction.

**What does "passiver Rechtsschutz" (passive legal protection) mean at LVM?**
LVM notifies counterparties that it will handle all correspondence itself and removes its customer from the exchange. This has no bearing on your direct claim against the liability insurer under §115 VVG.

**Is LVM a "good" claims handler?**
Its 2024 BaFin ratio of 0.76 is the best in the industry. Documented individual cases exist — misquotations, passive legal protection manoeuvres — but the overall complaint density is below average.
