# DA Direkt: Claim Settlement, Corporate Structure and Your Rights

> **In brief:** DA Direkt (DA Deutsche Allgemeine Versicherung AG) is a subsidiary of the **Zurich Group** — not, as frequently assumed, of Generali. Documented since 2011 are expert fee reductions citing the standard justification "not required within the meaning of § 249 BGB". The claims operation is shared with Zurich. The BaFin motor insurance complaint ratio for 2024 is not reported separately for DA Direkt. This hub corrects the corporate misattribution and shows how to secure your claims.

## How DA Direkt Settles Third-Party Claims

First, an important **correction of a widespread misconception**: DA Direkt belongs to the **Zurich Group Germany**, not to Generali. It is routinely confused with CosmosDirekt, which is in fact the Generali direct subsidiary. In practical terms, this means DA Direkt uses the same claims infrastructure as Zurich (LOGICHECK, ControlExpert, DEKRA).

The documented signature position is the **reduction of expert fees (Sachverständigenhonorar) citing the standard justification that these are "not required within the meaning of § 249 BGB"** — on record since 2011. The AG München (343 C 7350/10, 09.11.2011) consolidated six joined proceedings and awarded the extra-judicially reduced expert costs in full. The AG Siegburg (113 C 63/13, 24.07.2013) ordered DA Direkt to pay a reduced expert fee of 74.20 €; the AG Koblenz (161 C 611/13, 29.07.2013) ordered payment of the remaining expert costs.

Under legal pressure, DA Direkt — like Zurich — frequently invokes BGH rulings on expert fees, but interprets them in favour of its own reduction policy. Once a claim is filed with the court, an acknowledgement of liability often follows. A separate BaFin motor insurance complaint ratio for 2024 is not reported for DA Direkt.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against DA Direkt

1. **Reject the "not required" argument.** The costs of a necessary expert report (Gutachten) are recoverable under established BGH case law (§ 249 BGB). The blanket justification "not required" does not support a reduction — multiple courts have confirmed this.
2. **Scrutinise BGH citations.** Have the actual holdings of any cited rulings independently verified before accepting a reduction.
3. **Set a payment deadline.** A specific payment deadline triggers statutory default under § 286 BGB upon expiry.
4. **Enforce through our partner law firm.** Since an acknowledgement of liability frequently follows once court proceedings are initiated, legal pressure is the fastest route — where liability is clear, DA Direkt bears the legal costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does DA Direkt belong to Generali?**
No — this is a widespread misconception. DA Direkt (DA Deutsche Allgemeine Versicherung AG) is a subsidiary of the Zurich Group. The Generali direct subsidiary is CosmosDirekt.

**Is DA Direkt permitted to reduce my expert fee as "not required"?**
No, not as a blanket measure. The costs of a necessary expert report are recoverable under BGH case law (§ 249 BGB). The AG München (343 C 7350/10) awarded the reduced expert costs in full.

**Who handles my claim at DA Direkt?**
The claims operation is shared with Zurich (LOGICHECK, ControlExpert, DEKRA). The registered office is in Oberursel; claims processing runs through the Zurich location in Köln.
