# Allianz: Claim Settlement, ControlExpert, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** Allianz Versicherungs-AG has held a majority stake in the claims-review provider ControlExpert since March 2020 (German Federal Cartel Office clearance on 21 October 2020, case B9-49/20). In documented third-party claims, reductions are frequently based on ControlExpert review reports, and rental car costs are reduced to the Fraunhofer benchmark via LOGICHECK. This hub sets out the relationship plainly and highlights the case law that supports your claims.

## How Allianz Settles Third-Party Claims

The defining feature is **Allianz's ownership stake in the claims-review provider ControlExpert**: Allianz X announced the acquisition of a majority interest on 09 March 2020; the Federal Cartel Office cleared the transaction on 21 October 2020 (B9-49/20). This means a company majority-owned by a competitor reviews repair invoices — including those of claimants insured with other insurers. The Amtsgericht Hagen (16 C 371/05, 24 May 2006) found at an early stage that ControlExpert's calculations considered "only a fictitious hourly rate, entirely detached from the actual accident damage."

For **rental car costs**, the review provider LOGICHECK is used to benchmark the standard rate against the Fraunhofer rental price index — even where established local case law provides otherwise (as documented by the German car rental association, bav.de). Since 2024, lawyers have additionally reported **expert-fee reductions** on the grounds that the appraiser is not a BVSK member.

**The dispute style** is more formal than HUK-Coburg's and more strongly focused on proprietary claims management ("active claims management" with collection and delivery service as the first point of contact). In documented cases, pre-litigation settlement offers in the region of around 50% of the amount in dispute have been observed; after service of a statement of claim, a settlement is frequently reached shortly before the oral hearing. The Landgericht Frankfurt (Oder) (72 O 10/15, 18 July 2016) found that delaying tactics were the cause of an increased Nutzungsausfall (loss of vehicle use) at Allianz's expense.

In the Forsa survey of 1,072 traffic law attorneys (AG Verkehrsrecht im DAV, Stern 49/2017), Allianz ranked **third** — with **44%** of lawyers reporting frequent difficulties — behind HUK-Coburg (68%) and VHV (46%). The BaFin complaint rate for 2024 stands at 1.77 per 100,000 policies, below the industry average of 2.2; lawyers' experience is considerably more critical than the raw complaint figures, which is partly attributed to the ongoing discussion around ControlExpert.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against Allianz

1. **Contextualise the review report.** A ControlExpert review report is not a neutral expert opinion — it is a position paper in the insurer's interest, produced by a company the insurer majority-owns. Your independent Gutachten (expert report) remains the authoritative document.
2. **Counter the rental car reduction.** Do not let the LOGICHECK reduction go unchallenged: the applicable standard is the locally prevailing standard rate. Both the Schwacke index and the Fraunhofer index are recognised in principle as valid estimation bases, with the trial court retaining discretion under § 287 ZPO.
3. **Set a deadline and trigger default.** Set a payment deadline; once it expires, Allianz is in default under § 286 BGB. Pre-litigation offers of around 50% are an opening position, not adequate compensation.
4. **Enforce through a lawyer.** Where liability is clear, Allianz bears your legal costs as well. Legal pressure shortens the path to the settlement that is often offered only at a late stage.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is ControlExpert and what does Allianz have to do with it?**
ControlExpert is a review provider for automotive repair invoices. Allianz has held a majority stake since 2020 (Federal Cartel Office clearance B9-49/20). A ControlExpert review report is therefore not a neutral expert opinion — it is a position paper in the insurer's interest.

**Can Allianz reduce my rental car costs via LOGICHECK?**
Allianz may review whether costs were necessary, but the applicable standard is the locally prevailing standard rate. Both the Schwacke and Fraunhofer indices are permissible estimation bases; a blanket reduction to the Fraunhofer benchmark that runs counter to local case law is open to challenge.

**Allianz is offering me roughly half — should I accept?**
In documented cases, pre-litigation partial offers are an opening position, not adequate compensation. With an independent expert report and legal representation, the outstanding difference can typically be recovered.

**How does Allianz perform in BaFin complaint statistics?**
The BaFin complaint rate for 2024 stands at 1.77 per 100,000 policies (232 complaints), below the industry average of 2.2. In lawyers' experience, however — Forsa 2017: third place — the assessment of Allianz is considerably more critical.
