# AXA: Claim Settlement, Regress Wave, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** AXA Versicherung AG has pursued an aggressive approach in documented cases since 2022: expert fees are reduced on the basis of LOGICHECK audit reports — and, unusually for the industry, AXA issues retroactive regress demands against damage appraisers. The BaFin complaint ratio for 2024 stands at 2.27 per 100,000 contracts, above the industry average of 2.2. This hub provides a clear-eyed analysis of these practices and sets out the judgments that support your claims.

## How AXA Settles Third-Party Claims

Two patterns characterise the documented cases. The first is **expert fee reductions based on audit reports**: the specialist service LOGICHECK provides the calculation underpinning the reduction. The AG Heinsberg (18 C 32/22, 11.04.2022) rejected one such LOGICHECK-based fee reduction.

The second — unusual in the industry — is the **retroactive regress demand against damage appraisers**: according to the specialist publication IWW ("Unfallregulierung effektiv", under the heading "Regresswelle"), AXA has since 2022 been demanding that appraisers return portions of fees already reimbursed, on the grounds that it was obliged to pay the injured party the full — but allegedly excessive — costs, and is now reclaiming what it considers the unjustified portion. For injured parties this is primarily relevant indirectly — it reveals AXA's fundamental stance on expert fees.

**The litigation style** is described in case commentary as "going silent": no response to solicitors' letters, sometimes even in the face of an existing OLG ruling on the very matter (captain-huk.de, "Dreist, dreister, AXA"). A response frequently comes only after service of a statement of claim, at which point the full amount is often settled "as a gesture of goodwill." The AG Düsseldorf (36 C 10926/12, 02.08.2013) rejected an AXA workshop referral; the AG Frankfurt (32 C 2787/15, 19.10.2015) ruled in favour of injured parties on notional billing, incidental expenses, and expert fees.

The **BaFin complaint ratio** for 2024 stands at 2.27 per 100,000 contracts (100 complaints), above the industry average. In the Franke & Bornberg motor insurance tariff rating 2024, 32 AXA tariffs received only a "satisfactory" grade; within the VEMA broker network, AXA dropped from 3rd to 10th place for individual motor risks.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against AXA

1. **Do not accept silence.** If there is no response, set a clear deadline and formally establish default under § 286 BGB. The documented pattern of "response only after litigation" points strongly to early escalation through a lawyer.
2. **Your own expert report sets the benchmark.** A LOGICHECK-based fee reduction is an insurer's position. What governs is the value established in your independent expert report (§ 249 BGB).
3. **Be aware of appraiser regress.** If your expert receives a regress demand from AXA, this has no effect on your own reimbursement claim — raise this with your lawyer.
4. **Enforce through legal counsel.** Where liability is clear, AXA bears your legal costs. Given the "goodwill settlement after litigation" pattern, court proceedings are often the fastest route to full payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is AXA not responding to my solicitor's letter?**
In documented cases, the absence of a pre-litigation response is a recurring pattern; a reply typically comes only after service of a statement of claim. Early deadline-setting and legal representation are therefore advisable — where liability is clear, AXA bears the costs.

**What is the AXA regress wave against appraisers?**
According to the specialist publication IWW, AXA has since 2022 been demanding that damage appraisers return portions of fees already paid. This has no effect on your own reimbursement claim under § 249 BGB.

**Is AXA permitted to reduce my expert fee via LOGICHECK?**
AXA may challenge it, but your independent expert report remains the binding standard. The AG Heinsberg (18 C 32/22) rejected a LOGICHECK-based reduction.
