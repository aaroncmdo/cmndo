# R+V: Claim Settlement, Lawyer Questionnaire, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** R+V Allgemeine Versicherung AG is part of the cooperative DZ-BANK group and, at 0.77 complaints per 100,000 contracts, holds one of the lowest BaFin complaint ratios in the industry. Individual cases are nonetheless documented: a questionnaire sent to claimants containing leading questions about their choice of lawyer — against which the DAV formally protested — as well as Carexpert-backed reductions of expert fees. This hub separates verified individual cases from the overall below-average complaint density.

## How R+V Settles Third-Party Claims

The most widely discussed feature in legal practice is a **questionnaire sent to claimants represented by a lawyer**, containing leading questions such as "Did I engage the lawyer of my own accord, or was I told to instruct one?" DAV Executive Director Elmar Fuchs protested in writing to the R+V board in 2020 (versicherungsbote.de, "R+V-Schadenregulierung in der Kritik"). The questionnaire aims to call the reimbursability of legal costs into question — yet where liability is clear, those costs are reimbursable as consequential damages arising from default.

On individual claim items, R+V relies on **Carexpert audit reports**, for example regarding expert fees (Sachverständigenhonorar) and diminished value (Wertminderung). The Amtsgericht Nürnberg (20 C 10301/12, 31.03.2014) followed an independent court-appointed expert and awarded a Wertminderung that Carexpert had disputed; the Amtsgericht Frankfurt (32 C 3278/15, 22.10.2015) rejected an arbitrary reduction of expert fees.

**Important context (transparency):** These are documented individual cases. R+V's official BaFin complaint ratio stands at 0.77 per 100,000 contracts (36 complaints in 2024) — well below the industry average of 2.2. The complaint density is therefore below average. Even so, the same diligence is worthwhile in any claim as with any liability insurer.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against R+V

1. **Put the questionnaire in context.** You are not obliged to answer leading questions about your choice of lawyer. Where liability is clear, R+V bears your legal costs — free choice of lawyer is your right.
2. **A Carexpert audit report is not an expert report (Gutachten).** Your independent expert report under §249 BGB (German Civil Code on damage compensation) remains the authoritative basis.
3. **Set a deadline.** A specific payment deadline triggers default under §286 BGB once it lapses.
4. **Seek legal representation.** R+V frequently shows willingness to pay following a lawyer's letter; litigation is less often necessary than with more aggressive insurers.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I have to complete R+V's questionnaire about my lawyer?**
No. You are not required to answer leading questions about your choice of lawyer. Where liability is clear, your legal costs are reimbursable, and choosing your own lawyer is your right.

**Is R+V a "bad" claims settler?**
Its 2024 BaFin complaint ratio of 0.77 is one of the lowest in the industry. There are documented individual cases (the questionnaire, Carexpert reductions), but the complaint density is below average.

**May R+V reduce my expert fees via Carexpert?**
R+V may challenge them, but your independent Gutachten (expert report) remains authoritative. Courts have overturned R+V reductions — see, for example, AG Frankfurt 32 C 3278/15.
