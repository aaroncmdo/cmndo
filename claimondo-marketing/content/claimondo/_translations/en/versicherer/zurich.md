# Zurich: Claim Settlement, Rental Car Reductions, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** Zurich Deutschland has documented patterns of reducing rental car costs using the Fraunhofer rental price index and cutting expert fees with standard "necessity" arguments. On the positive side, Zurich offers its own online form for injured parties. In 2024, the German branch was converted to Zurich Insurance Europe AG, which means a meaningful BaFin complaint ratio cannot yet be established. This hub shows you how to protect your claims.

## How Zurich Settles Third-Party Claims

The most frequently documented reduction involves **rental car costs**: the standard rate is benchmarked by the claims service provider LOGICHECK against the Fraunhofer rental price index — even in cases where local case law supports higher rates. The AG Iserlohn (40 C 159/09, 21.09.2009) ruled in a Zurich case in favour of the Schwacke index over Fraunhofer — both benchmarks are generally permissible, with the court exercising discretion under § 287 ZPO.

For **expert fees (Sachverständigenhonorar)**, Zurich reduces payments using standard "necessity" (Erforderlichkeit) arguments. The AG Frankfurt (31 C 646/12, 10.05.2012) and AG Bonn (101 C 292/12, 07.03.2013) both ruled in favour of the injured parties. Once a claim is filed, Zurich frequently acknowledges the claim in full or reaches a settlement.

**Worth noting positively:** Zurich provides its own **online form for injured parties and claimants** — a transparent step that is rare among German insurers. An important corporate clarification: **DA Direkt is a sister brand within the Zurich Group** (not, as commonly assumed, a Generali subsidiary); both brands share the same claims infrastructure.

On the BaFin situation: due to the **2024 branch conversion** (from Zurich Insurance plc to Zurich Insurance Europe AG, NL Deutschland), the complaint ratio is not yet meaningful; 31 complaints have been recorded for the new branch, and the dataset is still being built.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against Zurich

1. **Challenge rental car reductions.** Do not accept a LOGICHECK/Fraunhofer reduction without response. The applicable standard is the locally prevailing standard rate; the AG Iserlohn enforced the Schwacke index in a Zurich case.
2. **Use the claimant form — but hold your ground.** The online form makes reporting easier; your claims under § 249 BGB (German Civil Code on full compensation) remain unchanged.
3. **Set a deadline.** Specify a concrete payment deadline; once it passes, Zurich is in default under § 286 BGB.
4. **Enforce through our partner law firm.** Once a claim is filed, a settlement or acknowledgement frequently follows — legal pressure shortens the process, and where liability is clear, Zurich bears the legal costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Zurich reduce my rental car costs using the Fraunhofer index?**
Zurich is entitled to review whether costs were necessary (Erforderlichkeit), but the applicable benchmark is the locally prevailing standard rate. Both Schwacke and Fraunhofer are permissible; the AG Iserlohn enforced the Schwacke index in a Zurich case.

**Does DA Direkt belong to Zurich or Generali?**
To the Zurich Group. DA Direkt is a sister brand of Zurich and shares the same claims infrastructure — it is not, as commonly assumed, a Generali subsidiary (that is CosmosDirekt).

**Why does Zurich have no meaningful BaFin complaint ratio for 2024?**
Due to the branch conversion to Zurich Insurance Europe AG (2024), the dataset is still being established; no reliable ratio can yet be calculated (31 complaints recorded).
