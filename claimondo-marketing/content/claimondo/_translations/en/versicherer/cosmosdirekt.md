# CosmosDirekt: Claim Settlement, the Generali Apparatus, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** CosmosDirekt is the direct subsidiary of Generali Deutschland, headquartered in Saarbrücken, and Germany's largest direct insurer. Third-party claims are processed through the Generali claims apparatus. Documented cases show long processing times and extended telephone wait times; the workshop-binding obligation applies through the WerkstattservicePLUS tariff. The BaFin motor insurance complaint ratio for 2024 is not reported separately. This hub explains your rights as the injured party (Geschädigter).

## How CosmosDirekt Handles Third-Party Claims

CosmosDirekt is not an independent third-party claims handler in the conventional sense — it is a **direct subsidiary of Generali**, and all processing runs through the Generali apparatus. As a purely online and telephone-based insurer with no field agents, user reports (Finanztip, motor-talk.de) document **long telephone wait times and multi-month processing periods** in the event of a claim.

One important clarification concerns service promises: the occasionally advertised "payment within 7 days, or €50 extra" applies **only to CosmosDirekt's own comprehensive (Kasko) policyholders** — not to third parties injured under a liability claim. As the injured party of a CosmosDirekt policyholder, you hold the standard direct claims against the liability insurer under § 115 VVG.

The **workshop-binding obligation** under the WerkstattservicePLUS tariff applies to CosmosDirekt's own policyholders (a premium discount in exchange for using partner workshops). It does not apply to you as an injured third party — your free choice of workshop and expert appraiser (Sachverständiger) remains fully intact. For individual claim items, the Generali-typical review apparatus (Carexpert) is applied.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against CosmosDirekt

1. **Use the direct claim.** Under § 115 VVG, you can assert your claims directly against the liability insurer — regardless of the direct-insurer model.
2. **Keep your free choice.** Workshop-binding obligations and service promises concern only CosmosDirekt customers, not you as the injured party.
3. **Set a deadline and document default.** For prolonged processing: set a firm payment deadline; once it lapses, default (Verzug) applies under § 286 BGB.
4. **Escalate to legal representation early.** Given documented delays, early engagement of a lawyer is advisable — where liability is clear, the opposing party bears the costs.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is CosmosDirekt part of Generali?**
Yes. CosmosDirekt (Cosmos Versicherung AG, Saarbrücken) is the direct subsidiary of Generali Deutschland; third-party claims are processed through the Generali apparatus.

**Does the "7-day promise" apply to me as an injured party?**
No. This promise applies only to CosmosDirekt's own comprehensive (Kasko) policyholders. As an injured third party, you hold the standard claims against the liability insurer under § 115 VVG.

**Do I have to use a partner workshop?**
No. The WerkstattservicePLUS binding obligation applies only to CosmosDirekt customers. Your free choice of workshop and expert appraiser (Sachverständiger) as the injured party remains fully intact.
