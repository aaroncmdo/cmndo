# HUK-Coburg Allgemeine: Claim Settlement, Reduction Practices, and Your Rights

> **In brief:** HUK-Coburg Allgemeine AG is Germany's largest motor liability insurer — and recorded the highest BaFin complaint rate among the twelve largest providers in 2024, at 2.73 complaints per 100,000 policies. Documented cases repeatedly involve reduced hourly labour rates, denied Wertminderung (diminished value), and payment only after court proceedings have been initiated. This hub sets out the documented practice in plain terms and identifies the court rulings that support your claims.

## How HUK-Coburg Settles Third-Party Claims

The defining pattern in documented cases is the **reduction of the expert's fee (Sachverständigenhonorar)** — typically on the grounds that the invoiced amount exceeds an internal benchmark the insurer applies unilaterally. The legal portal Captain HUK has documented several hundred court rulings on HUK-Coburg reductions since 2006, the majority concerning expert fees and associated costs (photo flat fees, travel expenses, administrative charges).

Also characteristic is **intervention in the repair invoice via audit report**: hourly labour rates, UPE surcharges, transfer costs, and blending costs are frequently disallowed when claims are settled on a notional basis. The LG Düsseldorf (20 S 109/18, 15.02.2019) rejected a reduction of €1,675.74 based on a ControlExpert audit report — an audit report is not a neutral expert opinion but a position statement commissioned by the insurer.

**The dispute style** is predominantly written and template-driven. In case documentation from lawyers (Kanzlei Schleyer, unfall-lexikon.de), clients described a recurring escalation pattern: pre-litigation, outstanding payments are frequently withheld; only after a claim is served on the policyholder is the remaining amount typically settled at short notice. The AG Coburg (15 C 696/17, 14.07.2017) noted it was "irritated to observe that general principles of damages law … were being negated to the detriment of an accident victim."

This perception aligns with the legal profession's external view: in a Forsa survey of 1,072 road traffic lawyers commissioned by the AG Verkehrsrecht of the DAV (October/November 2017, Stern 49/2017), **68%** named HUK-Coburg as the insurer with which problems in liability settlement arose "frequently" — the highest figure in the survey. An important caveat: this is a professional-sample survey, not an objective measure, but it is corroborated by hundreds of documented individual rulings.

The high absolute complaint figure (310 motor complaints in 2024) is partly a function of sheer market volume — HUK-Coburg is the largest group in the market. The **BaFin rate** normalises for this: at 2.73 complaints per 100,000 policies, HUK-Coburg Allgemeine nonetheless stood above the industry average of 2.2 and at the top of the top-12 in 2024.

## How to Enforce Your Claims Against HUK-Coburg

1. **Commission your own expert.** Under § 249 BGB, as a party who is not at fault you have the right to an independent expert of your own choosing — the cost is borne by the opposing liability insurer. Do not accept a referral to a mere repair estimate.
2. **Do not treat the audit report as the final word.** A reduction based on an audit report is the insurer's position, not an expert opinion. Insist on the values established in your own Gutachten (expert report).
3. **Set a deadline.** Set a specific payment deadline (the standard is four weeks from receipt of the complete supporting documents). Once that deadline passes, default occurs under § 286 BGB and default interest begins to accrue.
4. **Instruct a lawyer.** Where liability is clear, the opposing insurer bears your legal costs as well. Given the documented pattern of "payment only after proceedings are served," legal pressure materially increases the success rate.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why does HUK-Coburg only pay after court proceedings are initiated?**
In documented cases, full settlement is frequently withheld pre-litigation, while the outstanding amount is typically paid at short notice once proceedings are served. An independent Gutachten combined with legal representation shortens this process — in an accident that is not your fault, the opposing insurer bears the cost of both.

**Is HUK-Coburg permitted to reduce my expert's fee?**
The insurer may challenge the fee, but under consistent BGH case law the costs of a necessary expert report are a recoverable head of damage under § 249 BGB. Multiple courts have rejected HUK-Coburg's fee reductions — including the AG Köln (275 C 179/15).

**What is the audit report that HUK-Coburg sends?**
The audit report is a position statement produced by a service provider commissioned by the insurer — it is not a neutral expert opinion. The LG Düsseldorf (20 S 109/18) rejected a reduction based on exactly such a report. Your independent Gutachten remains the authoritative reference.

**What is HUK-Coburg's BaFin complaint rate?**
The 2024 BaFin statistics record a rate of 2.73 complaints per 100,000 policies for HUK-Coburg Allgemeine (310 complaints) — the highest figure among the twelve largest motor insurers and above the industry average of 2.2.
